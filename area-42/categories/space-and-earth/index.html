Space & Earth

Spaceflight, astronomy, planetary science, geophysics and models of Earth.

74 of 74 dossiers are indexed. Each entry evaluates a specific claim, documented plot, or alleged cover-up; unresolved events are not treated as proof.

DOSSIER // 001

A 2012 planetary alignment was supposed to destroy Earth

The 2012 planetary-alignment doomsday claim was false. Calendar rollover and apparent sky geometry posed no physical threat.

ALH 84001 as definitive fossil evidence from Mars

ALH 84001 preserves Martian history, but its alleged microfossils are not confirmed. A biological interpretation remains unproven, not concealed.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 003

“The Moon landings were staged”

Apollo missions allegedly never landed people on the Moon; photographs, shadows, flags, radiation, or video are presented as signs of a studio production.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 004

NASA hid an imminent Apophis impact risk

Apophis poses no impact threat in 2029, and current calculations rule out impacts for at least a century. Early uncertainty was publicly resolved.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 005

The asteroid belt is debris from a destroyed planet

The asteroid belt is a low-mass, dynamically stirred population, not the remains of a destroyed planet. Jupiter inhibited its growth; smaller collisions made asteroid families.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 006

The Event Horizon Telescope fabricated the black-hole image

The M87 image is a computational radio image based on real telescope data, not an ordinary snapshot. Its limitations are public; fabrication claims lack supporting evidence.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 007

The Chelyabinsk meteor was a nuclear strike

Chelyabinsk was a natural asteroid airburst, not a nuclear strike. Video, infrasound, and meteorites independently support that conclusion.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 008

The Chicxulub impact did not cause the dinosaur extinction

Chicxulub is a confirmed impact linked to the end-Cretaceous mass extinction. Volcanic contributions remain a legitimate research question, not evidence the impact was fabricated.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 009

A sudden crustal pole shift is being concealed

Rapid crustal pole shift is unsupported and conflicts with geodetic records. Plate motion, magnetic drift, and ancient true polar wander are distinct phenomena.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 010

A second natural moon is being hidden

Small temporary mini-moons are real, but no large hidden second Moon is known. The phenomenon is public and distinct from a secret stable satellite.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 011

Enceladus’ plume proves life has been found

Enceladus appears potentially habitable, but Cassini did not prove life. The discoveries are public geochemical findings, not a hidden detection.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 012

NASA found Europa’s ocean and is hiding it

A subsurface Europa ocean is strongly supported but indirect. It is neither a secret nor evidence that life has been found.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 013

Earth is rapidly expanding and plate tectonics is a cover story

Rapid Earth expansion is contradicted by geodetic and geological evidence. Small shape changes are real but unrelated to a growing planet.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 014

Flat Earth claims

Earth is said to be a flat plane, with photographs, satellite imagery, gravity, and spaceflight dismissed as fabricated or misinterpreted.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 015

The claim that Earth is stationary at the universe’s center

Earth-centered coordinates are convenient, but observations establish Earth’s rotation and orbit. No stationary-Earth physical model matches the full evidence better.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 016

A vast hollow Earth with an inhabited interior

A vast hollow Earth is incompatible with seismic waves, gravity, density, and the geomagnetic field. Natural cavities are tiny relative to the planet.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 017

The seismic inner core is actually a hollow cavity

A hollow inner core is contradicted by global seismology and geodesy. Details remain under study, but it is a dense central region.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 018

“Earth’s core stopped spinning”

The inner core did not simply stop, and Earth’s whole core remains active. Relative inner-core rotation is a subtle open question.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 019

Jupiter is an unambiguous shield protecting Earth

Jupiter reshapes small-body orbits, but its net effect on Earth’s impacts is model-dependent. Calling it an unambiguous shield oversimplifies evidence.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 020

NASA hid water found by the LCROSS impact

LCROSS publicly confirmed water-bearing material in one polar plume. A concealed lunar ocean or deliberate suppression is unsupported.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 021

The Moon’s far side is hidden to conceal a base

The far side is publicly mapped; relay requirements explain communication gaps. No credible evidence establishes a hidden base or global image cover-up.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 022

Transient lunar flashes as hidden activity

Recorded modern lunar flashes fit impacts; poorly documented historical events remain unclassified. None establishes a hidden installation.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 023

“Glass towers” and artificial lunar structures

No verified lunar tower or glass structure has been found. Viral examples are unsupported image interpretations, not evidence of concealment.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 024

An imminent magnetic pole reversal will cause catastrophe

Magnetic reversals are real, but evidence does not show one is imminent or would cause the claimed catastrophe.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 025

A hidden underground ocean beneath Earth’s crust

Earth may store substantial water in mantle minerals, but this is not a hidden liquid ocean. The discovery is public and its global scale uncertain.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 026

Martian “blueberries” as artificial objects

The “blueberries” are natural hematite-rich spherules. They support ancient groundwater activity, not manufactured objects or life.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 027

“NASA recolors Mars to hide its real sky”

Mars images are processed in documented scientific ways. The claim that processing conceals a blue sky or life is unsupported; raw data are public.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 028

The face on Mars at Cydonia

The Cydonia “face” is a natural mesa whose early appearance was amplified by lighting and low resolution. Public imagery contradicts a hidden monument.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 029

Mars methane detections as proof of a suppressed discovery

Mars methane remains an unresolved measurement and source problem. Evidence does not establish life or a cover-up.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 030

Seasonal oxygen on Mars as proof of life

The oxygen cycle is a real unresolved geochemical observation, not proof of life or suppression.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 031

The “Mars rat” in a rover photograph

The “Mars rat” is pareidolia, not evidence of an animal. Its photograph is public and supports no cover-up claim.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 032

Recurring slope lineae as hidden flowing water

Recurring slope lineae are observed, but current evidence does not establish flowing water or a cover-up. Dry granular activity explains many cases.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 033

Branching “trees” in Martian dune photographs

The branching streaks are frost-related surface processes, not trees. No credible evidence supports Martian vegetation in these images.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 034

The “canals” of Mars

Artificial Martian canals were a historical misinterpretation, not a real mapped feature. Mars’ ancient water evidence is geological.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 035

The Moon “rang like a bell,” so it is hollow

The Moon is not hollow. Apollo reverberations fit its dry, fractured interior, and independent geophysics supports a layered body.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 036

The Moon formed recently rather than billions of years ago

The Moon is ancient and formed early in Solar System history. Details of its giant-impact origin remain open; no evidence supports recent creation.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 037

The “Nemesis” companion star

The classic Nemesis companion is unsupported and strongly constrained by infrared and astrometric surveys.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 038

The 2012 Nibiru collision prediction

The 2012 collision claim is false: no Nibiru arrived, and no credible physical record supports the asserted approach.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 039

Phobos is a hollow artificial satellite

Phobos is a small, porous natural moon, not a proven hollow spacecraft. Its origin and internal structure remain open research questions.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 040

Planetary alignments trigger major earthquakes

Planetary alignments do not provide a demonstrated trigger or prediction method for major earthquakes. Weak tidal modulation is much narrower than the claim.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 041

“Planet Nine has already been found and hidden”

Planet Nine is unconfirmed. The claim that it has already been found and deliberately hidden lacks verifiable observations or records.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 042

Pluto’s 2006 status change was an openly debated definition prompted partly by new discoveries. The conspiracy claim is unsupported; terminology remains debated.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 043

Saturn’s north-pole hexagon is artificial

Saturn’s hexagon is a real atmospheric pattern, not an artificial structure. Its detailed dynamics remain an active scientific question.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 044

Saturn’s rings are young and their age is hidden

Saturn’s rings may be younger than the planet, but their age is uncertain and model-dependent. The question is public, not concealed.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 045

Titan’s methane lakes prove life was found

Titan’s hydrocarbon seas and organic chemistry are confirmed, but they are not proof of life. The claim of a concealed biological discovery is unsupported.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 046

Tunguska is best explained as a natural atmospheric airburst. The body’s precise nature is uncertain; nuclear or secret-weapon claims lack evidence.

Life in Venus’ clouds is unconfirmed and faces severe chemical challenges. The assertion it was proven and concealed is unsupported.

The 2020 phosphine result remains disputed and has not established phosphine or life on Venus. There is no evidence of a coordinated cover-up.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 049

Viking produced an unresolved chemical response, not definitive proof of life. Public evidence does not support a concealed discovery.

Vulcan is unsupported; general relativity explains Mercury’s anomalous precession, and no substantial intra-Mercurial planet has been detected.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 051

The pointed Antarctic mountains are natural glacial horns and nunataks; satellite perspective makes ordinary peaks resemble regular pyramids.

The Richat Structure is a deeply eroded geological dome in Mauritania, not an oceanic island or archaeological city matching Plato’s account.

Yellowstone has no predictable eruption schedule; monitoring records show no current signs of an imminent caldera-forming event.

READ THE DOSSIER →DOSSIER // 071
