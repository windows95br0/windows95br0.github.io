FILE TFF-02 // 1939 GERMAN-POLISH BORDER

The Gleiwitz Incident

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: The staging is well established; its exact command chain and strategic importance remain narrower questions.

Specific allegation

Claim: an SS team staged a radio-station attack and left a murdered prisoner as supposed evidence of Polish aggression.

Event chronology

On 31 August 1939, SS personnel seized Gleiwitz radio station, broadcast a brief Polish-language message, and left a prisoner’s body in attackers’ clothing. Germany invaded Poland the next morning.

Evidentiary record

Postwar testimony by SS officer Alfred Naujocks, trial evidence, and historical research converge on a staged incident among several border provocations. Germany’s invasion preparations predated the broadcast.

Independent and contrary evidence

Historians broadly accept staging; the remaining questions concern operational details and authorization. Naujocks’ account is corroborated but should not be accepted solely on his word.

Known uncertainty

The event was propaganda, not a genuine trigger of the invasion. Records are incomplete on the precise command chain and the victim’s identity.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

An authenticated SS order or corroborated operational log could clarify authorization. To overturn the staging account requires evidence of an actual Polish attack and disproof of the convergent perpetrator record.

Sources and limitations

  1. Nuremberg International Military Tribunal evidenceUsed to document the operation testimony and broader invasion chronology in the primary record. Limitation: it does not settle limits of postwar testimony and the exact command chain.
  2. USHMM, German Invasion of PolandUsed to compare the chronology and interpretation of the operation testimony and broader invasion chronology. Limitation: conclusions depend on limits of postwar testimony and the exact command chain.
  3. Alfred Naujocks testimony, Nuremberg recordUsed as an independent check on the operation testimony and broader invasion chronology. Limitation: it cannot alone establish limits of postwar testimony and the exact command chain.
