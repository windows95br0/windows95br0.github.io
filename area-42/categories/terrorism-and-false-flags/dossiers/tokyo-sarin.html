FILE TFF-26 // 1995 JAPAN

Tokyo Subway Sarin Attack

A source-led historical case file separates documented events, allegations, and unanswered questions. Uncertainty is not evidence for a particular explanation.

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: Aum responsibility is strongly established; state-staging claims lack credible evidence.

Specific allegation

Claim: a state or foreign actor staged the sarin attack; evidence instead connects production, operatives, and trials to Aum Shinrikyo.

Event chronology

Aum members released sarin on Tokyo subway trains on 20 March, killing 13 and injuring thousands. Police raids followed earlier crimes; trials prosecuted leaders and direct participants.

Evidentiary record

Japanese courts established Aum’s sarin production and roles. Chemical evidence, member testimony, and group facilities converge; public evidence shows no outside sponsor.

Independent and contrary evidence

Intelligence failure to connect warning signs is documented, but a missed signal does not prove complicity. Due-process criticism of state response does not negate the group evidence.

Known uncertainty

Not every technical record or internal authorization file is public. Leadership responsibility is reconstructed; victim totals vary with later deaths.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

External tasking, funds, and communications would be required to substantiate a foreign sponsor. Complete Aum records and agency files could clarify preventability.

Sources and limitations

  1. Supreme Court of Japan Aum judgmentsUsed to document convictions, investigative chronology, and technical capability in the primary record. Limitation: it does not settle limited public trial exhibits and a separate question of intelligence failure.
  2. Japan National Police Agency Aum investigationsUsed to compare the chronology and interpretation of convictions, investigative chronology, and technical capability. Limitation: conclusions depend on limited public trial exhibits and a separate question of intelligence failure.
  3. CNAS Aum weapons studyUsed as an independent check on convictions, investigative chronology, and technical capability. Limitation: it cannot alone establish limited public trial exhibits and a separate question of intelligence failure.
