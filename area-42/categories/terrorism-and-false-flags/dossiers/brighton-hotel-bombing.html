FILE TFF-16 // 1984 ENGLAND

Brighton Grand Hotel Bombing

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: The IRA attack is established; state foreknowledge is unsupported by public evidence.

Specific allegation

Claim: authorities had advance knowledge of the IRA bomb at the Conservative Party conference; public evidence shows penetration, not foreknowledge.

Event chronology

A time-delayed bomb planted weeks earlier exploded at the Grand Hotel on 12 October. Five people died; Margaret Thatcher survived. Patrick Magee was convicted, and the IRA claimed responsibility.

Evidentiary record

Court evidence, forensics, and the IRA claim establish the attack. The bomb’s long placement window exposed vulnerabilities; public files do not prove officials knew its location.

Independent and contrary evidence

General warnings and a successful security breach do not prove an official knowingly allowed the bombing. Participant memoirs require corroboration.

Known uncertainty

Some intelligence remains unavailable, and warning detail reaching hotel security is unclear. The exact placement sequence is reconstructed retrospectively.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

An authenticated warning naming the hotel or device, plus proof it reached and was suppressed by responsible officials, would support foreknowledge.

Sources and limitations

  1. UK Court of Appeal record, Patrick MageeUsed to document conviction evidence, chronology, and contemporaneous party records in the primary record. Limitation: it does not settle limited public access to intelligence and limits of claimed responsibility.
  2. BBC report on the Brighton bombingUsed to compare the chronology and interpretation of conviction evidence, chronology, and contemporaneous party records. Limitation: conclusions depend on limited public access to intelligence and limits of claimed responsibility.
  3. CAIN archive, conflict documentsUsed as an independent check on conviction evidence, chronology, and contemporaneous party records. Limitation: it cannot alone establish limited public access to intelligence and limits of claimed responsibility.
