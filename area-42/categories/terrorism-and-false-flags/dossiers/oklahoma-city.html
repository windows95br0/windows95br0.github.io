FILE TFF-25 // 1995 UNITED STATES

Oklahoma City Bombing

A source-led historical case file separates documented events, allegations, and unanswered questions. Uncertainty is not evidence for a particular explanation.

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: McVeigh and Nichols’ roles are established; a federal false-flag theory is unsupported.

Specific allegation

Claim: federal authorities staged or assisted the attack; trial evidence convicted Timothy McVeigh and Terry Nichols, while several witness leads stayed disputed.

Event chronology

A truck bomb destroyed the Murrah Federal Building on 19 April, killing 168. McVeigh was convicted and executed; Nichols was convicted. Investigators pursued inconsistent reports about another possible participant.

Evidentiary record

Trial exhibits and FBI records establish the truck, explosives, and principal perpetrators. No verifiable evidence displaced McVeigh and Nichols as central actors.

Independent and contrary evidence

Unresolved tips merit scrutiny but do not prove state sponsorship. No credible evidence shows federal agents made the bomb or staged the attack.

Known uncertainty

Some files remain redacted and witness descriptions conflict. Missing details are not proof of another bomber; not every old lead can be retested.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

An identified second participant with physical links to the truck, or authentic agency orders, could materially alter the account. New evidence must fit trial exhibits.

Sources and limitations

  1. U.S. Department of Justice trial recordsUsed to document prosecution evidence, official investigative account, and victim-centered chronology in the primary record. Limitation: it does not settle agency self-reporting and unresolved leads do not establish alternative perpetrators.
  2. FBI Oklahoma City bombing case historyUsed to compare the chronology and interpretation of prosecution evidence, official investigative account, and victim-centered chronology. Limitation: conclusions depend on agency self-reporting and unresolved leads do not establish alternative perpetrators.
  3. Oklahoma City National Memorial & Museum archiveUsed as an independent check on prosecution evidence, official investigative account, and victim-centered chronology. Limitation: it cannot alone establish agency self-reporting and unresolved leads do not establish alternative perpetrators.
