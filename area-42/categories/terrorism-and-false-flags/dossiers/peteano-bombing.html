FILE TFF-08 // 1972 ITALY

Peteano Bombing

A source-led historical case file separates documented events, allegations, and unanswered questions. Uncertainty is not evidence for a particular explanation.

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: A far-right bombing and investigation obstruction are documented; wider command claims are not established.

Specific allegation

Claim: a neo-fascist activist planted the bomb, while false evidence redirected investigators toward left-wing militants.

Event chronology

Three Carabinieri officers died when a booby-trapped car exploded near Peteano on 31 May. Vincenzo Vinciguerra later confessed; Italian courts examined fabricated evidence and obstruction in the investigation.

Evidentiary record

Court decisions and the confession support a neofascist perpetrator and specific obstruction by officials. Broader claims about a unified state terror command need separate evidence.

Independent and contrary evidence

A convicted participant can have self-serving motives, but key details were corroborated. The courts established particular acts of obstruction, not every later allegation about secret networks.

Known uncertainty

The affair involved secret security structures and incomplete records. Obstruction does not prove that all officials knew the true bomber or ordered the attack.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

Complete liaison files and authenticated orders could test higher-level direction. Proof officials knowingly promoted false leads after learning the truth would clarify intent.
