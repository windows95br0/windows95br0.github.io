FILE TFF-21 // 1988 SCOTLAND

Pan Am Flight 103 / Lockerbie

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: The bombing is certain; legal attribution to Libya is substantial but contested and does not settle all planners.

Specific allegation

Claim: Libya’s legal attribution is disputed by Iran-retaliation and alternative-perpetrator theories; unresolved concerns do not prove a false attribution.

Event chronology

A bomb destroyed Flight 103 over Lockerbie on 21 December, killing 270. Scottish investigators traced a suitcase device. Al-Megrahi was convicted; a second Libyan defendant was acquitted.

Evidentiary record

Trial and appeal records detail the forensic case. A review commission identified possible miscarriage concerns; the conviction was upheld. Iran-backed theories were investigated but not proved in court.

Independent and contrary evidence

The legal conclusion is not universal historical consensus. Evidence disclosure and witness reliability are legitimate questions, but do not establish a specific alternative.

Known uncertainty

Key forensic interpretations and baggage routing remain contested. Al-Megrahi’s death prevented later cross-examination; accomplices and motive are not fully settled.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

Full lab notes, baggage records, and files on alternatives could be compared. New evidence must explain trial-tested findings, not infer attribution from geopolitics.

Sources and limitations

  1. High Court of Justiciary trial judgmentUsed to document the trial evidence, independent case review, and appellate history in the primary record. Limitation: it does not settle review concerns do not themselves overturn the conviction or prove an alternative.
  2. Scottish Criminal Cases Review CommissionUsed to compare the chronology and interpretation of the trial evidence, independent case review, and appellate history. Limitation: conclusions depend on review concerns do not themselves overturn the conviction or prove an alternative.
  3. Scottish appeal and review materialsUsed as an independent check on the trial evidence, independent case review, and appellate history. Limitation: it cannot alone establish review concerns do not themselves overturn the conviction or prove an alternative.
