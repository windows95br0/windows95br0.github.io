FILE TFF-23 // 1993 UNITED STATES

1993 World Trade Center Bombing

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: The bombing and cell are established; FBI orchestration is unsupported.

Specific allegation

Claim: FBI informant recordings show agents controlled or allowed the attack; trial evidence established a cell led by Ramzi Yousef.

Event chronology

A truck bomb detonated beneath the World Trade Center on 26 February, killing six. Investigators convicted cell members. Recordings later revealed informant Emad Salem’s contacts with FBI handlers and bomb-making discussions.

Evidentiary record

Court records establish the cell and bomb. Salem’s recordings document a controversial relationship, but his proposed bomb was not the device used in the attack.

Independent and contrary evidence

Informant mismanagement and missed opportunities merit scrutiny, but do not show the FBI built or approved the bomb. Available evidence does not establish a false flag.

Known uncertainty

Handler decisions were not fully aired at trial, and recordings surfaced later. The record cannot reconstruct every possible chance to disrupt the cell.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

Complete recordings and logs, plus evidence linking Bureau-supplied materials to the bomb used, could change the view. Timing and physical traces are falsifiable checks.

Sources and limitations

  1. U.S. appellate decision, United States v. SalamehUsed to document court evidence, prosecution theory, and independent exposure of informant issues in the primary record. Limitation: it does not settle the informant controversy is not a finding that agents made the bomb.
  2. U.S. Department of Justice case materialsUsed to compare the chronology and interpretation of court evidence, prosecution theory, and independent exposure of informant issues. Limitation: conclusions depend on the informant controversy is not a finding that agents made the bomb.
  3. New York Times reporting on Emad Salem tapesUsed as an independent check on court evidence, prosecution theory, and independent exposure of informant issues. Limitation: it cannot alone establish the informant controversy is not a finding that agents made the bomb.
