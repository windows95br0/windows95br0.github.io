FILE TFF-49 // 2014 UKRAINE

Malaysia Airlines Flight MH17

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: A missile downing is established; Dutch-led attribution is supported by extensive technical and judicial records, while every command level is not resolved.

Specific allegation

Claim: Flight MH17 was a false-flag attack by Ukraine or another actor; technical and court inquiries found it was downed by a missile fired from separatist-held territory.

Event chronology

On 17 July 2014, a missile destroyed Flight MH17 over eastern Ukraine, killing all 298 aboard. Dutch-led investigators traced the weapon system’s movement and launch area; a Dutch court issued convictions in 2022.

Evidentiary record

The Dutch Safety Board established a Buk missile strike; the Joint Investigation Team traced the system to Russia and launch site near Pervomaiskyi. A court convicted three defendants in absentia, while the Russian government disputes attribution.

Independent and contrary evidence

The missile strike is beyond reasonable technical dispute. Competing state claims have changed repeatedly; a false-flag allegation must explain radar, debris, intercepts, and chain-of-custody evidence.

Known uncertainty

The court proceedings were in absentia for the convicted defendants, and senior command responsibility is not identical to direct launch responsibility. International legal proceedings continue.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

Independent access to original radar, missile components, chain-of-custody files, and authenticated communications could test attribution. Evidence must distinguish launch crew, command, and state responsibility.

Sources and limitations

  1. Dutch Safety Board, MH17 crash investigationUsed to establish technical cause, multinational criminal investigation, and court findings from the primary record. Limitation: it does not settle defendants were tried in absentia and broader command responsibility remains distinct.
  2. Joint Investigation Team public findingsUsed to compare chronology and interpretation of technical cause, multinational criminal investigation, and court findings. Limitation: conclusions depend on defendants were tried in absentia and broader command responsibility remains distinct.
  3. The Hague District Court MH17 judgmentUsed as an independent check on technical cause, multinational criminal investigation, and court findings. Limitation: it cannot alone establish defendants were tried in absentia and broader command responsibility remains distinct.
