FILE TFF-39 // 2013 UNITED STATES

Boston Marathon Bombing

A source-led historical case file separates documented events, allegations, and unanswered questions. Uncertainty is not evidence for a particular explanation.

CALIBRATED CONCLUSION: The brothers’ responsibility is established in court; government orchestration is unsupported.

Specific allegation

Claim: the bombing was a government operation or staged exercise; public court and investigative records attribute the attack to two brothers without proof of a false flag.

Event chronology

Two pressure-cooker bombs exploded near the Boston Marathon finish line on 15 April 2013, killing three and injuring hundreds. A police pursuit ended in the death of one suspect and capture of the other.

Evidentiary record

Dzhokhar Tsarnaev was convicted after a federal trial; evidence included surveillance video, physical evidence, and witness testimony. A separate commission reviewed intelligence sharing and response.

Independent and contrary evidence

Prior tips about one brother were investigated but did not establish imminent operational intent. The presence of exercises or security drills is not evidence of staging absent a causal link.

Known uncertainty

The surviving defendant’s appeal and sentencing process concern legal issues, not every intelligence failure. Investigators’ account is well supported, though agencies can still be scrutinized.

Falsifiable evidence requirements

Raw surveillance footage, device forensics, and original intelligence referrals are testable records. A staging claim would require evidence that officials built or directed the bombs, not merely conducted training.
