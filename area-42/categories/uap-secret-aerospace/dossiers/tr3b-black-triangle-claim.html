UAP-065 // CASE REVIEW

“TR-3B” black-triangle craft allegation

No verifiable records support the alleged TR-3B; secrecy alone does not establish exotic technology.

The precise claim

The “TR-3B Astra” is described in popular accounts as a secret triangular aircraft using exotic or anti-gravity propulsion, sometimes attributed to reverse-engineered extraterrestrial technology.

Chronology and provenance

The name became prominent in late-20th-century UFO and alternative-aerospace publications, rather than in a verifiable public procurement record or authenticated aircraft manual. Later internet accounts repeat technical descriptions and alleged sightings, often without traceable original documents.

Public records and observations

No publicly authenticated U.S. government aircraft record, manufacturer publication, test report, or independently inspected hardware substantiates the TR-3B as described. The existence of genuine classified aircraft such as the F-117 and B-2 is documented, but no public record links those programs to the claimed name or anti-gravity system.

Evidence limits

The claim’s source chain is weak: repeated anonymous accounts are not independent corroboration, and alleged specifications are not accompanied by test data, parts, or verifiable contracts. Classified programs can limit access, but that possibility cannot serve as positive evidence for a particular vehicle.

Conventional alternatives

Conventional triangular aircraft, helicopters, formation lights, perspective, and hoaxes can explain individual reports. Actual stealth aircraft are a grounded category; the extraordinary propulsion and TR-3B identity remain unsupported. Each sighting needs its own evidence rather than being assigned to a label.

Calibrated skeptical conclusion

The TR-3B as an exotic operational craft is an unsupported allegation, not a confirmed classified aircraft. Historical secret aircraft programs do not validate the TR-3B or concealment claims.

What could change this assessment?

An authenticated procurement or flight-test document, attributable engineer testimony corroborated by records, independently examined hardware with clear custody, and reproducible propulsion tests could change this assessment.
