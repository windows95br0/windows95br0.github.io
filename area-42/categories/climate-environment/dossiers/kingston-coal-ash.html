DOSSIER A42-CE-59 // CLIMATE & ENVIRONMENT

Kingston: coal-ash dike failure

The allegation

A TVA impoundment dike failed in 2008, releasing about 5.4 million cubic yards of ash. The issue is engineering and oversight, not deliberate release.

Timeline

Failure occurred Dec. 22, 2008. EPA oversaw cleanup; TVA reported river ash removal complete in 2015. EPA adopted national rules in 2015.

What the record establishes

TVA OIG and EPA document failure, cleanup, and response. Ash contains hazardous constituents but exposure depends on location and route.

Counterevidence and alternative explanations

Monitoring did not show uniform community exposure. One non-detect cannot prove no exposure elsewhere.

Uncertainty and limits

Dose and health estimates are uncertain because exposure varied and cleanup disturbed ash.

Calibrated skeptical verdict

Failure and oversight shortcomings are established. Deliberate release or proven nearby epidemic is not.

What could change the assessment

Independent ash checks, long-term monitoring, worker records, and health follow-up could revise risks.
