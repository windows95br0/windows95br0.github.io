DOSSIER A42-CE-76 // CLIMATE & ENVIRONMENT

Aliso Canyon: prolonged methane storage leak

The allegation

A gas-storage well leaked methane and associated chemicals for nearly four months in 2015–16. Claim: safety oversight and response were inadequate. Emissions are measured; intentional release or proven chronic community illness is not.

Timeline

Leak began Oct. 23, 2015, was publicized later that month, and was plugged in Feb. 2016. Thousands of households relocated; safety reforms followed.

What the record establishes

CARB estimates, aircraft measurements, and peer-reviewed analysis quantify a major methane release. State investigations examined well integrity and oversight.

Counterevidence and alternative explanations

Methane’s climate effect is clear; local health concerns involve co-emitted compounds. Symptom reports are real but nonspecific; relocation reduced exposure.

Uncertainty and limits

Pre-event baseline and individual exposure records are limited. Mixtures and follow-up complicate chronic effect estimates.

Calibrated skeptical verdict

Prolonged uncontrolled leak and serious system failures are established. Specific chronic health causation remains uncertain; intentional release is unsupported.

What could change the assessment

Well-integrity records, independent emissions monitoring, health follow-up, and storage-field audits could refine exposure and responsibility.
