DOSSIER A42-CE-55 // CLIMATE & ENVIRONMENT

Times Beach: dioxin-contaminated waste oil

The allegation

Dioxin waste oil was sprayed on roads in Times Beach. The question is whether handlers and agencies controlled and disclosed hazards, not deliberate experimentation.

Timeline

EPA found high dioxin in 1982; flooding prompted evacuation. A federal buyout followed in 1983; soil was incinerated in the 1990s.

What the record establishes

EPA records document sampling, evacuation, buyout, and cleanup. ATSDR assessed exposure pathways and health data.

Counterevidence and alternative explanations

Soil contamination was uneven; animal reports were not a town-wide human study. Flood risk and uncertain extent favored removal.

Uncertainty and limits

Historical samples and personal exposure records are limited; dose and route shape risk.

Calibrated skeptical verdict

Contamination, evacuation, and cleanup are established. A deliberately suppressed town-wide epidemic is not established.

What could change the assessment

Property data, waste invoices, agency correspondence, and long-term cohort studies could clarify responsibility and health effects.
