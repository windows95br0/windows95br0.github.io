FILE FCP-61 / HISTORICAL CASE FILE

HealthSouth earnings manipulation and Richard Scrushy’s acquittal

ASSESSMENT: HealthSouth fraud is documented; Scrushy was acquitted of federal accounting charges. Saying he was convicted of that fraud is false.

Documented findings, economic interpretation and unresolved claims are assessed separately.

Claim under review

Did executives falsify accounts to meet targets, and was founder Richard Scrushy criminally responsible?

Chronology

Former CFOs admitted a multi-year scheme; it became public in March 2003. A federal jury acquitted Scrushy of every count in June 2005. Later civil and state proceedings were separate; later bribery conviction concerned another matter.

Court, regulatory and primary evidence

CFO pleas, restatements and SEC action establish accounting manipulation. The federal acquittal is primary legal evidence: Scrushy was not convicted of the accounting fraud.

Independent cross-checks

Independent corroboration: DOJ Scrushy acquittal (2005) provides a separate record relevant to this case. Official federal jury outcome.

Competing account and limits

Cooperators received plea benefits; acquittal means proof beyond reasonable doubt failed, not that his account was necessarily true. Civil burden differs.

Evidence that could alter this assessment

Underlying entries, instructions and any appellate civil rulings could refine his knowledge, but not erase the federal verdict.

Skeptical finding

HealthSouth fraud is documented; Scrushy was acquitted of federal accounting charges. Saying he was convicted of that fraud is false.
