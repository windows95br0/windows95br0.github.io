FILE FCP-67 / HISTORICAL CASE FILE

SAC Capital insider trading and firm liability

ASSESSMENT: Several insider-trading cases and firm plea are established; not every employee or position was illegal.

Documented findings, economic interpretation and unresolved claims are assessed separately.

Claim under review

Did SAC-associated managers trade on material nonpublic information, and was the firm itself criminally liable?

Chronology

Several traders pleaded guilty or were convicted. SAC Advisors pleaded guilty to securities and wire fraud in 2013, forfeited $900m and paid $1.2bn. Mathew Martoma was convicted in 2014.

Court, regulatory and primary evidence

DOJ plea and facts establish entity liability; individual cases tie specific tips and trades to named traders. SEC civil settlement separately concerns firm controls.

Independent cross-checks

Independent corroboration: SEC SAC settlement (2013) provides a separate record relevant to this case. Civil firm-control resolution.

Competing account and limits

Not all trades were prosecuted; materiality and knowledge require specific proof. Corporate guilt does not automatically establish every employee’s guilt.

Evidence that could alter this assessment

Tip-source messages, market disclosure times, trade logs and appeals could alter unadjudicated episodes.

Skeptical finding

Several insider-trading cases and firm plea are established; not every employee or position was illegal.
