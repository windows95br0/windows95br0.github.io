FILE FCP-69 / HISTORICAL CASE FILE

Luckin Coffee fabricated sales and U.S. listing

ASSESSMENT: Fabricated sales are strongly documented; exact losses and each executive’s role remain specific questions.

Documented findings, economic interpretation and unresolved claims are assessed separately.

Claim under review

Did Luckin fabricate 2019 sales and expenses to mislead investors?

Chronology

Nasdaq listing began in 2019. After Jan. 2020 short-seller report, company disputed allegations; April internal investigation found fabricated transactions beginning Q2 2019, about RMB 2.2bn. SEC settled for $180m in Dec.; company delisted.

Court, regulatory and primary evidence

Luckin’s internal report and SEC complaint corroborate fake sales and investor misstatements; court entered settlement. Short-seller estimates were not all confirmed.

Independent cross-checks

Independent corroboration: Luckin special committee announcement (2020) provides a separate record relevant to this case. Company’s account of investigation.

Competing account and limits

Settlement without admission or denial is not trial verdict; individual liability differs. Later restructuring does not validate 2019 reports.

Evidence that could alter this assessment

Executive cases, payment/coupon records and store-level sales reconciliation could revise amounts and responsibility.

Skeptical finding

Fabricated sales are strongly documented; exact losses and each executive’s role remain specific questions.
