FILE FCP-54 / HISTORICAL CASE FILE

FTX customer assets, Alameda and Sam Bankman-Fried

ASSESSMENT: Customer assets were misused and Bankman-Fried’s specified fraud convictions stand; avoid generalizing to every employee or creditor.

Documented findings, economic interpretation and unresolved claims are assessed separately.

Claim under review

Did FTX divert customer deposits to Alameda and misrepresent safeguards and financial condition?

Chronology

A withdrawal crisis triggered FTX bankruptcy on Nov. 11, 2022. A jury convicted Bankman-Fried on seven fraud/conspiracy counts in Nov. 2023; he was sentenced to 25 years in March 2024. Bankruptcy recoveries use separate valuation rules.

Court, regulatory and primary evidence

Trial messages, ledgers, testimony and verdict establish Bankman-Fried’s guilt on specified counts. Bankruptcy court declarations trace company funds independently.

Independent cross-checks

Independent corroboration: SEC, Bankman-Fried complaint (2022) provides a separate record relevant to this case. Regulatory allegations distinct from criminal verdict.

Competing account and limits

Defense challenged intent and cooperating witnesses; repayment estimates do not erase convictions. Verdict does not decide each employee’s culpability or every creditor’s recovery.

Evidence that could alter this assessment

Appellate rulings and a complete reconciliation of deposits, transfers and authorizations could revise amounts and attribution.

Skeptical finding

Customer assets were misused and Bankman-Fried’s specified fraud convictions stand; avoid generalizing to every employee or creditor.
