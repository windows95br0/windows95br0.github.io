FILE FCP-65 / HISTORICAL CASE FILE

Standard Chartered Iran sanctions transactions and compliance

ASSESSMENT: Repeated sanctions violations are established within specified entities and transactions; no broad geopolitical conspiracy is shown.

Documented findings, economic interpretation and unresolved claims are assessed separately.

Claim under review

Did Standard Chartered process Iran-linked U.S.-dollar transactions and conceal information from regulators?

Chronology

NY DFS raised concerns in 2012; bank resolved with multiple regulators for about $667m. In 2019 it admitted further sanctions violations involving Iran and other jurisdictions and paid additional penalties.

Court, regulatory and primary evidence

DFS consent order, DOJ plea, OFAC and Federal Reserve actions cover distinct periods and transactions. 2019 plea establishes the charged entity’s guilt for admitted conduct; 2012 has separate posture.

Independent cross-checks

Independent corroboration: NY DFS 2012 consent order provides a separate record relevant to this case. Primary state regulatory order.

Competing account and limits

Sanctions rules changed over time; bank said it strengthened controls after 2012, while later action found failures. Legality depends on dates, parties, routing and licenses.

Evidence that could alter this assessment

SWIFT messages, screening records, monitor findings and compliance certifications could refine period and individual knowledge.

Skeptical finding

Repeated sanctions violations are established within specified entities and transactions; no broad geopolitical conspiracy is shown.
