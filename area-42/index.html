Area 42:
the claim file.

A skeptical field guide to popular conspiracy narratives. Each file separates what is documented, what is disputed, and what the available evidence does not establish. This is an educational reference—not an endorsement of any allegation.

STATUS: OPEN SOURCE REVIEW // NO CLAIM GAINS CREDIBILITY MERELY BY BEING SECRET, UNEXPLAINED, OR POPULAR.

ANALYST'S RULE

Start with the strongest, most specific version of a claim. Ask what observation would distinguish it from ordinary explanations, then look for evidence that could prove it wrong.

EVIDENCE FIRST
CONFIDENCE LAST

[ HOW TO READ THIS FILE ]

A claim is a starting point.

01 / DOCUMENTED

Supported by primary records, repeatable observation, or converging independent evidence.

02 / UNRESOLVED

A real observation or historical gap exists, but its cause or interpretation remains uncertain.

03 / NOT ESTABLISHED

The available evidence does not support the allegation, or the claim makes predictions that fail checks.

Official reports are important sources, not infallible authorities. Compare their underlying data and methods with independent scrutiny. A correction to one detail does not automatically validate a much larger theory.

FILE A42-01 // SKY OBSERVATIONS

UAP, UFOs & claims of visitation

The claim

Some accounts interpret unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) as craft of non-human origin, secretly recovered technology, or evidence of a coordinated cover-up.

What the record supports

People and sensors do report objects or events that are initially unidentified. “Unidentified” describes the state of available information, not a conclusion about origin. Public investigations have attributed many cases to balloons, satellites, aircraft, sensor artifacts, or insufficient data; some remain unresolved.

Assessment: UAP reports are a legitimate subject for careful investigation. Public evidence does not establish extraterrestrial visitation or a recovered alien craft. Lack of resolution is not positive evidence for a specific extraordinary explanation.

Useful checks

  • Request original sensor data, metadata, calibration, viewing geometry, and chain of custody—not only a compressed clip or witness summary.
  • Compare independent sensors and mundane explanations, including parallax, glare, focus, range-estimation error, and classified human aircraft.
  • Separate first-hand observation from retelling; ask whether a claim can be independently tested.
  1. NASA — Unidentified Anomalous PhenomenaIndependent study materials and report; discusses data limitations and research recommendations.
  2. U.S. All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office — Historical Record Report, Volume IGovernment historical review; read its scope and evidentiary method critically.
  3. National Academies — Scientific study of aerial phenomena (workshop materials)Scientific discussion of data collection and analysis limits.

FILE A42-02 // LUNAR EXPLORATION

“The Moon landings were staged”

The claim

Apollo missions allegedly never landed people on the Moon; photographs, shadows, flags, radiation, or video are presented as signs of a studio production.

What the record supports

The Apollo missions are supported by extensive engineering and mission records, tracking, returned lunar samples, independent observations, and hardware left on the lunar surface. Individual photographs can be confusing without understanding exposure, perspective, reflectance, and the lunar environment.

Assessment: The hoax claim conflicts with multiple independent lines of physical and historical evidence. Common photo “anomalies” are not evidence of staging unless a proposed explanation survives the full photographic and engineering context.

Useful checks

  • Check whether a claim predicts a measurable result that differs between a landing and a studio simulation.
  • Compare complete mission photography and transcripts rather than isolated, context-free images.
  • Distinguish the documented Apollo landings from reasonable debate about program cost, risk, politics, and individual mission details.
  1. NASA — Apollo landing sitesLunar reconnaissance imagery of Apollo sites and hardware.
  2. NASA Johnson Space Center — Apollo lunar sample collectionCatalog and scientific context for returned lunar samples.
  3. NASA History OfficeMission histories, documents, and primary-source archives.

FILE A42-03 // 22 NOVEMBER 1963

The JFK assassination

The claim

Alternative theories allege a second gunman or involvement by intelligence agencies, organized crime, political rivals, or foreign governments. The theories are not one unified account.

What the record supports

The official investigations reached different conclusions in some areas. The Warren Commission concluded Lee Harvey Oswald acted alone; the 1979 House Select Committee concluded a conspiracy was probable, relying significantly on acoustic evidence later disputed by the National Research Council. A large documentary record exists, but not every record resolves every question.

Assessment: The historical record contains genuine uncertainties and contested investigative findings. Those facts warrant archival research; they do not by themselves identify a second shooter or prove a named organization directed the assassination.

Useful checks

  • Trace each assertion to the underlying document, recording, physical evidence, or testimony and note when it was created.
  • Distinguish a demonstrated inconsistency from an inference about motive or coordination.
  • Read later re-evaluations of the acoustic evidence alongside the original committee finding.
  1. U.S. National Archives — JFK Assassination Records CollectionPrimary records, release information, and collection guide.
  2. Warren Commission — ReportOfficial 1964 investigation and appendices.
  3. National Research Council — Report of the Committee on Ballistic AcousticsLater review of the acoustic evidence used in the HSCA conclusion.

FILE A42-04 // 11 SEPTEMBER 2001

September 11 attacks & demolition claims

The claim

Some narratives allege the attacks were orchestrated or enabled by U.S. officials, or that explosives—not aircraft impact and fires—caused the World Trade Center collapses.

What the record supports

The 9/11 Commission documented the al-Qaeda plot and intelligence failures. NIST's engineering investigations analyzed the WTC building collapses and reported no corroborating evidence for controlled demolition. Critiques should address the full engineering record, not only footage that appears counterintuitive.

Assessment: Government failures and later policy decisions are documented subjects for scrutiny. They are not proof of foreknowledge or orchestration. Publicly available evidence does not substantiate the controlled-demolition claim.

Useful checks

  • Separate the questions of who planned the attacks, what agencies knew, how buildings failed, and how governments responded.
  • Check structural claims against engineering analysis, construction details, fire data, and physical evidence.
  • Account for the difference between an official investigation's limitations and affirmative evidence for a rival theory.
  1. National Commission on Terrorist Attacks Upon the United States — Final ReportCommission report and supporting materials.
  2. NIST — World Trade Center investigation FAQsInvestigation scope, engineering findings, and responses to common questions.
  3. NIST — World Trade Center investigation archiveReports, technical documents, and data from the structural investigation.

FILE A42-05 // ATMOSPHERIC OBSERVATION

Contrails & “chemtrails”

The claim

Ordinary-looking aircraft trails are alleged to be secret chemical or biological spraying, often inferred from trail persistence, grid patterns, or later cloud cover.

What the record supports

Contrails form when hot, moist engine exhaust mixes with cold air; persistence depends on upper-atmosphere humidity and temperature. Aircraft emissions and deliberate weather-modification research are real subjects, but they do not establish a covert, large-scale spraying program.

Assessment: Trail appearance alone cannot establish composition, intent, or a coordinated program. The available public evidence does not support the widespread secret-spraying allegation. Specific pollution or geoengineering proposals should be assessed separately with measurements and records.

Useful checks

  • Compare flight routes and upper-air meteorological conditions with the time and location of the observation.
  • For a chemical claim, require validated sampling, controls, chain of custody, and independent replication—not a photograph or nonspecific soil test.
  • Ask whether ordinary contrail physics predicts the observed persistence and spreading.
  1. U.S. EPA — Contrails and clouds formed by aircraftAtmospheric science overview and response to common claims.
  2. Federal Aviation Administration — ContrailsAircraft and atmospheric explanation.
  3. National Academies — Climate intervention researchSeparate, explicit discussion of proposed sunlight-reflection research and governance.

FILE A42-06 // PLANETARY GEOMETRY

Flat Earth claims

The claim

Earth is said to be a flat plane, with photographs, satellite imagery, gravity, and spaceflight dismissed as fabricated or misinterpreted.

What the record supports

Earth's approximate oblate-spheroid shape is supported by geodesy, navigation, time zones, satellite tracking, eclipses, long-distance observations, and measurements made with independent methods. Local surfaces look flat because Earth's curvature is small at everyday scales.

Assessment: The flat-Earth model does not account for the combined, independently reproducible observations as well as the measured globe model. A single visual perspective or an imperfect image is not a substitute for a coherent model that makes accurate predictions.

Useful checks

  • Repeat measurements at different locations and times, such as shadow-angle comparisons, star altitude, or horizon observations.
  • Compare predictions for solar illumination, lunar eclipses, long-distance routes, and satellite passes.
  • Use known survey methods and instrument uncertainty; do not treat perspective, refraction, or camera lenses as negligible.
  1. NOAA Ocean Service — Is the Earth round?Earth's shape and measurement overview.
  2. NOAA National Geodetic SurveyGeodetic reference systems, coordinates, and measurement resources.
  3. NASA — Earth observationsSatellite and Earth science data and mission context.

FILE A42-07 // ATLANTIC OCEAN

The Bermuda Triangle

The claim

A loosely bounded part of the western North Atlantic is said to cause a disproportionate number of mysterious ship and aircraft disappearances through paranormal, magnetic, or extraterrestrial forces.

What the record supports

The region has heavy traffic, difficult weather, powerful currents, and ordinary navigation and mechanical hazards. There is no universally accepted official boundary, and famous stories may omit weather, route, vessel condition, or later corrections.

Assessment: The popular claim of a uniquely dangerous or paranormal zone is not established by reliable comparative statistics. A disappearance can remain unresolved without requiring an extraordinary cause.

Useful checks

  • Check original incident reports, dates, vessel identity, weather, route, and whether later evidence changed the story.
  • Compare risk rates using consistent exposure data (traffic, distance, vessel type, period), not a list of dramatic cases.
  • Account for the Gulf Stream, storms, shallow reefs, and ordinary limits of historical search-and-rescue records.
  1. NOAA Ocean Service — What is the Bermuda Triangle?Summary of the region's status and proposed explanations.
  2. U.S. Coast Guard — Bermuda Triangle informationCoast Guard perspective on the alleged anomaly.
  3. NOAA National Hurricane CenterHistorical and current tropical weather information for the region.

FILE A42-08 // DOCUMENTED SECRET PROGRAM

MKUltra & mind-control narratives

The claim

Because a real secret CIA program conducted unethical human experiments, some narratives extend the record to claim reliably perfected mind control, remote behavioral control, or specific modern events directed by the program.

What the record supports

MKUltra was a documented CIA research program involving drugs and other methods. The surviving record is incomplete in part because files were destroyed; congressional investigations documented serious ethical and oversight failures. That history supports scrutiny of state secrecy, not every later claim attributed to it.

Assessment: The program's existence and abusive practices are established. Claims of precise, dependable control over people or attribution of present-day events require their own evidence and are not established by the MKUltra record alone.

Useful checks

  • Distinguish what documents show from what an author infers from missing records.
  • Look for independently corroborated names, dates, budgets, facilities, and results.
  • Do not equate evidence that an agency acted secretly in one case with evidence that any alleged secret operation occurred.
  1. CIA Freedom of Information Act Electronic Reading Room — MKUltra collectionDeclassified records; incomplete archives require caution in both directions.
  2. U.S. Senate Select Committee — Project MKUltra, the CIA's Program of Research in Behavioral Modification1977 hearing record and recovered documentation.
  3. U.S. National Archives — Rockefeller Commission recordsRecords relating to U.S. intelligence activities and oversight.

FILE A42-09 // RESTRICTED TESTING RANGE

Area 51 & secret aircraft

The claim

Secrecy around the Nevada test site is taken as evidence of alien craft, extraterrestrial bodies, or technology reverse-engineering.

What the record supports

The U.S. government has used the remote range for classified aircraft and defense testing. Declassified histories connect unusual mid-century sightings in part to high-altitude U-2 and OXCART flights. Restricted access and classified programs are real; public evidence of alien hardware at the site has not been independently verified.

Assessment: A secret test site is not proof of a particular secret. The documented aviation history explains why secrecy and unusual sightings became linked, but does not resolve every report or establish an extraterrestrial program.

Useful checks

  • Separate confirmed classified aerospace work from unverified stories about non-human technology.
  • Require a verifiable artifact, provenance, independent examination, and reproducible analysis for physical claims.
  • Treat anonymous testimony as a lead to investigate, not as a substitute for corroboration.
  1. CIA — The Central Intelligence Agency and Overhead ReconnaissanceDeclassified context for U-2, OXCART, and high-altitude reconnaissance.
  2. National Museum of the U.S. Air Force — Lockheed U-2Aircraft history and technical context.
  3. All-domain Anomaly Resolution OfficePublic reports and records concerning UAP claims; review scope and evidence carefully.

[ FINAL NOTE // SOURCE HYGIENE ]

What would change the assessment?

Good investigations are revisable. A strong new claim should make clear what evidence would count against it, publish enough methodology for independent checking, preserve original data, and distinguish direct observation from inference. Anonymous claims, missing files, and unexplained events can justify more research; they cannot, by themselves, identify a culprit or prove an extraordinary explanation.
