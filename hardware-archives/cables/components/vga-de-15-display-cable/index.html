VGA DE-15 Display Cable

Analog RGB video cable standard. This entry records a distinct product or well-defined family; differences among revisions and similarly named models are called out below.

Recorded specifications

Cataloged partVGA DE-15 Display Cable
Era / familyAnalog RGB video cable standard
Key specifications15-pin DE-15 connector; separate RGB analog channels, horizontal/vertical sync, DDC pins; shielding and conductor layout affect signal.
Interface / fitVGA carries analog video only; passive VGA-to-HDMI does not work without active conversion and often separate power/audio.
Variants / identificationConnector is often called “DB-15” colloquially; DE-15 is the correct shell family designation.
