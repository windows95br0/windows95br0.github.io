StarTech USB2HAB2M 2 m USB 2.0 Type-A-to-Type-B Cable

USB 2.0 Type-A-to-Standard-B male cable; 2 m; up to 480 Mb/s. This record is SKU-specific; connector shape alone does not establish protocol or capability.

CLASSIFICATION: Data, peripheral, and high-speed cables. Verify exact SKU and the connected host/peripheral requirements.

SKU-specific interface and capability

Exact product SKUStarTech.com USB2HAB2M
Generation and connector pinoutUSB 2.0 Type-A male to USB 2.0 Standard-B male, 4 contacts at each end: VBUS, D−, D+, and GND. The B plug is not the larger 9-contact USB 3 Standard-B plug. Pinout summary: 4-wire USB 2.0 Type-A-to-Standard-B: VBUS, D−, D+, GND; shield continuity depends on assembly.
Length and construction2 m, passive copper cable.
Signaling rateUSB 2.0 High-Speed up to 480 Mb/s; USB 1.1 low/full-speed modes (1.5/12 Mb/s) remain endpoint-dependent. No SuperSpeed lanes are present.
Power limitsUSB 2.0 standard downstream port limit is up to 5 V / 500 mA after configuration (and lower before configuration); the cable has no independent power supply. The connected host and device control actual available current.
Compatibility limitsFor a USB-A host and a USB-B peripheral such as a printer, DAC or enclosure that supports the appropriate USB 2.0 mode. USB 3 capability is unavailable even if a device has a visually similar connector.

Compatibility & handling

  • Identify both receptacle types, the exact SKU, protocol generation and direction before connecting; similar-looking USB connectors can carry different pinouts and capabilities.
  • Maximum rate and power are ceilings, not guarantees: the full host/cable/peripheral chain must support and negotiate the same mode.
  • Do not use the lead to defeat a port’s current limit, connect unlike power pinouts, or assume charging/video support from connector fit.
