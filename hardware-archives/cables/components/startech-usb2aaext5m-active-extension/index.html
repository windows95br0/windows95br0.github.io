StarTech USB2AAEXT5M 5 m Active USB 2.0 Extension Cable

USB-A male-to-female active extension; 5 m; USB 2.0 High-Speed. This record is SKU-specific; connector shape alone does not establish protocol or capability.

CLASSIFICATION: Data, peripheral, and high-speed cables. Verify exact SKU and the connected host/peripheral requirements.

SKU-specific interface and capability

Exact product SKUStarTech.com USB2AAEXT5M
Generation and connector pinoutUSB Type-A male upstream to USB Type-A female downstream; four USB 2.0 contacts (VBUS, D−, D+, GND). Active extension electronics preserve USB 2.0 signaling over the extension segment. Pinout summary: Four-contact USB 2.0 A-to-A extension path: VBUS, D−, D+, GND; shielding and active repeater arrangement are product-specific.
Length and construction5 m (16 ft) extension segment; active, not a passive coupler.
Signaling rateUSB 2.0 High-Speed up to 480 Mb/s, with lower-speed compatibility according to the host/device; no USB 3.x SuperSpeed or USB-C Alt Mode.
Power limitsUSB 2.0 standard downstream port is limited to 5 V / up to 500 mA after configuration; the product is not USB-PD. Follow the manufacturer installation instructions for any optional power arrangement and do not assume a longer run increases available current.
Compatibility limitsMale A end connects upstream to a host/hub; female A end connects a downstream USB 2.0 device/cable. Preserve direction. Do not use as a USB 3 extension or chain beyond the manufacturer’s supported configuration.

Compatibility & handling

  • Identify both receptacle types, the exact SKU, protocol generation and direction before connecting; similar-looking USB connectors can carry different pinouts and capabilities.
  • Maximum rate and power are ceilings, not guarantees: the full host/cable/peripheral chain must support and negotiate the same mode.
  • Do not use the lead to defeat a port’s current limit, connect unlike power pinouts, or assume charging/video support from connector fit.
