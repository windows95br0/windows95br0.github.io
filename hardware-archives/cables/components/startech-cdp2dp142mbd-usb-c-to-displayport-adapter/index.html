StarTech CDP2DP142MBD USB-C-to-DisplayPort 1.4 Adapter

A one-way display adapter from a USB-C source that supports DisplayPort Alternate Mode to a DisplayPort display. USB-C shape alone does not guarantee video output.

IDENTIFICATION / SAFETY: Match the complete part number, connector direction, host requirements, and revision to manufacturer documentation. A matching connector shape is not proof of protocol or electrical compatibility.

Compatibility & electrical hazards

Requires a USB-C host port with DisplayPort Alt Mode (or another explicitly supported video mode). It is not a bidirectional DisplayPort-to-USB-C adapter and does not add video capability to a data-only USB-C port. Verify source GPU, mode, display, and adapter revision.

Archival & handling notes

Label the USB-C end as source and DisplayPort end as display. Record the host model and negotiated mode when testing; protect the small USB-C connector from side load and debris.

For collection records, photograph both connector faces and all labels; record manufacturer, complete SKU, revision, provenance, condition, and the source document used for identification. Do not apply power to an unidentified or damaged cable.
