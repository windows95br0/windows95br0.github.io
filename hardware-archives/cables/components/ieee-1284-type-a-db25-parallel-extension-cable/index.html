[ CABLE / ADAPTER RECORD // IEEE 1284 TYPE A HOST CABLE ]

IEEE 1284 Type A DB-25 Parallel Extension Cable

DB-25 straight-through parallel wiring for host/peripheral or extension use; data/control/status pins remain separate parallel signals.

CLASSIFICATION: Legacy printer and parallel cables. Identify protocol, direction, pinout and exact cable revision before connection.

Interface and capability

Standard / protocolIEEE 1284 Type A host cable. Connector appearance alone does not establish compatibility.
Endpoint / pinoutDB-25 straight-through parallel wiring for host/peripheral or extension use; data/control/status pins remain separate parallel signals.
Capability / limitsMode performance and usable total cable length depend on IEEE 1284 compliance and endpoints; avoid chained extensions.
Certification / lengthStandards define interface requirements, not every assembly’s performance. Certification, mode, current/power rating, direction and maximum length belong to the exact product/revision and complete channel; verify its manufacturer specification. Do not infer certification from connector shape or marketing terminology.

Compatibility & handling

  • This does not convert to USB or change protocol. Prefer one shielded, compliant cable; verify gender and pin continuity.
  • Inspect connector keying, latch, contact condition, shielding, jacket and model/revision marking. Stop if contacts are bent, loose, scorched, corroded or mismatched.
  • Route without tension or sharp bends; follow product bend-radius, strain-relief, current and environmental limits. Test at the intended protocol and mode—physical fit is not a compatibility test.
