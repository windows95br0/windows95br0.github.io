Model specifications

Exact model / topologyAPC / Schneider Electric SRT8KXLT; Online double-conversion UPS for 208 V single-phase service.
Rated output8000 VA / 8000 W. VA and W limits are separate constraints; never exceed either.
Input / output voltageInput: 208 V nominal; hardwired input. Output: 208 V nominal output; confirm exact site wiring and configured settings in the installation guide.
Input connectionHardwired input connection (no plug/receptacle); 208 V installation by qualified personnel.
Output receptacles4 × NEMA L6-20R and 2 × NEMA L6-30R output receptacles.
Battery chemistry / serviceInternal sealed lead-acid battery system; runtime may be extended with compatible SRT external battery packs. Exact pack and replacement procedure are model-specific.
RuntimeUse the manufacturer SRT8K/SRT10K load-runtime documentation for the exact load and external-pack setup. Actual runtime depends on battery age/condition, charge, ambient temperature and configuration.
Transfer behaviorContinuous online double-conversion operation; no normal utility-to-inverter transfer delay. Bypass transfers are protective/operating modes, not a promise of uninterrupted output during every fault or overload.
Communications / compatibilityUSB and serial interfaces; SmartSlot/network management accessories are supported as configured. Check card compatibility and its installation/safety requirements.
Compatibility / safety208 V hardwired supply, 8 kVA/8 kW output and L6-20R/L6-30R loads only. Requires correctly rated dedicated wiring, grounding and overcurrent protection; installation/service by qualified electrical personnel under local code. Read the exact revision of the manufacturer manual before connection, service or battery replacement.

Service and compatibility notes

  • Measure and record connected watts and source voltage; runtime is not guaranteed for an aged or untested battery.
  • Do not open energized mains equipment. Never defeat protective earth, overload an outlet, use damaged equipment, or substitute an unapproved battery.
  • For hardwired or high-current versions, qualified installation, rated branch protection and local electrical code apply.
  • Confirm the complete model suffix, input wiring, output voltage, outlet map and manual revision against the unit nameplate before connecting equipment.
