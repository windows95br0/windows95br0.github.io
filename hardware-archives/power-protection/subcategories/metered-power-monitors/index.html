Metered power monitors

Power and energy measurement hardware includes plug-in monitors, circuit sensors, DIN-rail meters, panel instruments and portable loggers. Metering is distinct from backup or electrical protection; confirm sensors, measured metrics and communications for the exact model. Each product has an individual record and original local schematic.
