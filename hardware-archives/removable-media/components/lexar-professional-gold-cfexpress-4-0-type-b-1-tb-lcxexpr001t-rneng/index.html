Lexar Professional GOLD CFexpress 4.0 Type B 1 TB (LCXEXPR001T-RNENG)

The 1 TB LCXEXPR001T-RNENG belongs to Lexar’s newer GOLD CFexpress 4.0 Type B line and is distinct from the older GOLD 128 GB CFexpress 2.0 card in this catalog. This entry describes the removable card only; the camera or reader is separate.

MEDIA ONLY: Lexar Professional GOLD CFexpress 4.0 Type B 1 TB (LCXEXPR001T-RNENG). Capacity, interface generation, and compatibility refer to this model and not other cards in the same family.

Capacity, interface and compatibility

Manufacturer modelLCXEXPR001T-RNENG; CFexpress 4.0 Type B, Professional GOLD
Nominal capacity1 TB. Usable space varies with formatting and system-reserved capacity.
InterfacePCIe 4.0 x2 / CFexpress 4.0 Type B.
PerformanceLexar rates this 1 TB GOLD 4.0 product up to 3,600 MB/s read, up to 3,300 MB/s write, and 3,000 MB/s sustained write. Peak speed requires a compatible CFexpress 4.0 reader/host.
Host compatibilityA device that supports CFexpress Type B and the 1 TB capacity. A Type B connector alone does not guarantee CFexpress 4.0 speeds; consult the camera/reader compatibility list and firmware notes.
Write protectionNo physical card-level write-protect switch is specified for this model; use host controls and verified backups.
Model-specific noteThis is the 4.0 1 TB GOLD model, not the separate GOLD CFexpress 2.0 128 GB record. Lexar notes card transfer speeds vary with reader and host capability.

Handling and preservation

No finite shelf-life or archival-life guarantee is claimed here. Keep the card clean, dry, labeled, and in its protective case; avoid touching contacts and never remove it during a write. After use, verify files and maintain multiple checksummed copies on independent media. Before a migration or recovery, record the exact card SKU, camera/reader, firmware, filesystem, and host.
