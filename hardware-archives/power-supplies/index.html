[ COMPONENT RECORD // POWER CONVERSION ]

Power
supplies.

A computer PSU converts mains AC into regulated DC outputs for the system. It is a safety-critical component: ratings, protection features, connectors, and cable pinouts must all match the build.

CLASS RECORD: ATX-style desktop PSUs. Modular cables are not universally interchangeable, even when plugs physically fit. Never open a PSU; internal capacitors can retain hazardous charge.

Reference specifications

InputAC input-voltage range, frequency, and current are printed on the unit label and vary by model/region. Use the exact label rather than assuming universal input.
OutputsRegulated DC rails typically include +12 V, +5 V, +3.3 V, and standby power; usable combined rail limits are specified on the PSU label.
Connectors24-pin motherboard, 4/8-pin CPU EPS, SATA power, legacy peripheral, and PCIe GPU connectors. Newer products may use 12V-2x6; verify PSU and GPU requirements.
RatingsRated continuous output in watts, efficiency certification, protections, dimensions, and acoustic behavior are model-specific. Peak and continuous ratings are not interchangeable.

Selection & safe service

  • Estimate sustained system load and leave suitable headroom; check the GPU maker's PSU recommendation and transient-power requirements.
  • Use only the cables supplied for that exact PSU model/revision. Modular PSU-side pinouts are not standardized.
  • Seat high-current GPU connectors fully, avoid tight bends at the plug, and inspect for heat damage or discoloration.
  • Do not daisy-chain or adapt connectors outside the PSU/GPU manufacturer's instructions. Do not defeat over-current or ground protections.
  • For replacement, compare case length, fan orientation, connector count, rail ratings, and the official pinout/compatibility list.

Sources & further reading

  2. CLEAResult — 80 PLUS programEfficiency certification listings and program details; certification does not itself establish build quality.
