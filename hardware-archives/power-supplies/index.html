[ COMPONENT RECORD // POWER CONVERSION ]

Power
supplies.

A computer PSU converts mains AC into regulated DC outputs. Ratings, protections, connectors, and cables must match the system and the unit's exact revision.

CLASS RECORD: PC/XT, AT, ATX/ATX12V, SFX, TFX and Flex ATX supplies. Modular cables are not universally interchangeable, even when plugs physically fit. Never open a PSU; internal capacitors can retain hazardous charge.

Reference specifications

InputUse the exact AC voltage/frequency/current nameplate and regional manual.
OutputsDC rails and combined output limits are model-specific; see each individual record and nameplate.
Form factorsLegacy PC/XT/AT, ATX/ATX12V, SFX/SFX-L, TFX and Flex ATX are electrically and mechanically distinct.
CompatibilityConnector shell, total wattage, and chassis fit alone do not establish electrical compatibility.

Selection & safe service

  • Verify exact model/revision, line input, DC-rail limits, connectors, pinout and dimensions using manufacturer documentation.
  • Use only modular cables listed for the exact PSU model/revision.
  • Never open or probe the PSU internally; hazardous voltages can persist after disconnection. Use qualified service or replace it.
