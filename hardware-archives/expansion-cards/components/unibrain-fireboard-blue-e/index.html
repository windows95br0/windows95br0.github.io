[ IEEE 1394 CONTROLLER // EXPANSION CARDS ]

Unibrain FireBoard Blue-e 1394a PCIe

OHCI PCIe FireWire 400 controller with two external and one internal connection. Verify the exact board label and revision before service.

MODEL SCOPE: Unibrain FireBoard Blue-e 1394a PCIe. Specification distinctions below identify 1394a/1394b rates, physical bus and power limitations; host OS and driver support can change by revision and release.

Specifications and compatibility

Exact model / SKUUnibrain FireBoard Blue-e bulk part 1213; Fire-i SDK-license part 1134.
Controller chipsetTexas Instruments TSB43AB23 1394 PHY/link controller plus TI XIO2001 PCIe-to-PCI bridge.
PCI bus / lanesPCI Express x1.
IEEE 1394a rateIEEE 1394a / FireWire 400, up to 400 Mb/s.
IEEE 1394b rateNot a 1394b / FireWire 800 adapter.
Ports and connectorsTwo external and one internal IEEE 1394a FireWire 400 ports (model specification); verify exact connector format against board revision.
Power / bus-power notesInternal 4-pin Molex auxiliary-power connector. Unibrain specifies up to 18 W Vbus per port and 54 W total; use the correct PSU lead and stay within the product rating.
OS driver compatibilityUnibrain documents Windows 7/8/10, macOS 10.4+ and Linux for the model; these historical version statements are not a guarantee for current releases. Use the platform’s OHCI driver and verify the intended application stack.
Host compatibilityPCI Express x1 host interface; check bracket/slot clearance, current OS IEEE 1394 OHCI support, device connector and power arrangement before installation.

Installation and archival notes

  • Match the card’s printed model/revision and connector layout to the cited maker documentation; do not infer a controller IC or current limit from the connector alone.
  • IEEE 1394 link rate is negotiated with the peripheral and cable. A FireWire 800 connector does not make a 400-Mb/s device or cable operate at 800 Mb/s.
  • Use ESD precautions; validate system bus wiring, driver version, peripheral power source, and application-level compatibility before replacing a working legacy host.
