[ IEEE 1394 CONTROLLER // EXPANSION CARDS ]

Delock 89155

Compact PCIe FireWire 400 card with two 6-pin and one 4-pin ports. Verify the exact board label and revision before service.

MODEL SCOPE: Delock 89155. Specification distinctions below identify 1394a/1394b rates, physical bus and power limitations; host OS and driver support can change by revision and release.

Specifications and compatibility

Exact model / SKUDelock 89155
Controller chipsetVIA VT6315N IEEE 1394 controller (Delock-listed chipset).
PCI bus / lanesPCI Express x1.
IEEE 1394a rateIEEE 1394a / FireWire 400, up to 400 Mb/s per link.
IEEE 1394b rateNot a 1394b / FireWire 800 adapter.
Ports and connectorsTwo external 6-pin IEEE 1394a ports and one external 4-pin IEEE 1394a port.
Power / bus-power notes6-pin connector power depends on card power circuitry and host supply; 4-pin FireWire is data-only. The product documentation does not list a separate auxiliary card-power socket.
OS driver compatibilityUse the operating system’s supported IEEE 1394 OHCI driver; Delock’s product documentation should be checked for supported OS/revision details. No claim of support for every current OS or legacy application is implied.
Host compatibilityPCIe x1 host slot; verify low-profile/full-height bracket fit, available slot clearance, OS OHCI driver and device power needs.

Installation and archival notes

  • Match the card’s printed model/revision and connector layout to the cited maker documentation; do not infer a controller IC or current limit from the connector alone.
  • IEEE 1394 link rate is negotiated with the peripheral and cable. A FireWire 800 connector does not make a 400-Mb/s device or cable operate at 800 Mb/s.
  • Use ESD precautions; validate system bus wiring, driver version, peripheral power source, and application-level compatibility before replacing a working legacy host.
