Philips Brilliance 329P1H

A 31.5-inch UHD business display combining a USB-C docking station, wired networking and a retractable Windows Hello-compatible webcam.

IDENTIFICATION: Philips Brilliance 329P1H; 2020 generation. Verify the full model code, region and hardware revision on the unit label. Published specifications describe the identified configuration; its matching manual and label take precedence if revisions differ.

Compatibility & connectivity notes

For one-cable docking, the host USB-C port must support DisplayPort Alt Mode; use the supplied/qualified cable for video, USB peripherals and up to 90 W charging. The webcam and Ethernet depend on the USB data connection, not video alone. Confirm webcam OS/Windows Hello requirements and host security policy; the 4K panel runs at 60 Hz.

Preservation notes

Record the complete model suffix, serial/date code, input selection, source device, cable or adapter, tested timing and observed condition. Retain the matching regional manual and any calibration or service records with the display. Follow the manufacturer’s cleaning and service instructions; do not open CRT equipment without appropriate high-voltage training.
