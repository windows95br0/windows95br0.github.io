Revision and model distinctionU2713HM is the standard-gamut model; it is distinct from the wide-gamut U2713H and their port/specification claims must not be interchanged. Verify the HM suffix and regional power label.

Host compatibility and connection notes

For full native resolution use DisplayPort or dual-link DVI-D with a host/GPU that supports 2560 × 1440 at 60 Hz. Dell documentation limits the HDMI input to lower timings than the native QHD mode; confirm the monitor guide and source output before choosing HDMI or an adapter.

This is a 60 Hz display without modern variable-refresh/HDR features. Treat any replacement panel, firmware, stand or power component as specific to the exact suffix/revision.

Preservation notes

Record the rear-label model, serial/date code, included stand/accessories, tested input, source timing, image condition and calibration state. Keep the matching manual and cable/adaptor chain with the unit; use qualified service procedures for internal work.
