Commodore Amiga 1011 External 3.5-inch Disk Drive

Model-specific archive record. Enclosure labels do not always identify the installed mechanism or host controller; preserve the full suffix and inspect the unit before connection or service.

MODEL IDENTIFICATION: Commodore Amiga A1011. Photograph the nameplate, serial, connector and power rating. Verify revision-specific instructions and inspect any replacement mechanism before use.

Recorded specifications

Exact modelCommodore Amiga A1011
Drive size / type3.5-inch external floppy drive.
Capacity / formats880 KB Amiga double-density format; not a PC 720 KB drive format.
Interface / controllerAmiga external floppy port, 23-pin D-sub; drive signals and host-supplied +5 V/+12 V are system-specific. No PC 34-pin cable.
PowerPowered by the compatible Amiga host through the 23-pin port; never apply separate power unless the model-specific manual says so.
Host OS / system compatibilityAmiga computers with a compatible external floppy port and AmigaDOS support; not directly compatible with PC/Mac systems.
Cable / connectorsCommodore Amiga external-drive 23-pin cable; verify full pinout, continuity and connector condition before connection.

Compatibility and preservation caveats

A1011 has no external pass-through on this model; it must be last in the chain. Do not rely on A1010-family assumptions. Verify nameplate/mechanism and avoid damaged media.

Inspect for corrosion, contamination, cracked plastics, damaged shutter, cable strain and prior repairs. Do not insert damaged media. Record host, OS, controller/driver, drive label, revision, media condition and acquisition settings. Preserve original images and document conversions separately.
