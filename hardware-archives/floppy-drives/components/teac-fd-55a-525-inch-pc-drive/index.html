TEAC FD-55A 5.25-inch PC floppy drive

The FD-55A is the single-sided, 40-track 48-TPI member of TEAC's FD-55 family. Do not apply double-sided FD-55B values or HD-family settings to this model.

Documented specifications

Density / capacitySingle-sided, 40 tracks at 48 TPI; FM single-density or MFM double-density media. PC formats are typically 160 KB (8 sectors) or 180 KB (9 sectors); not a double-sided 360 KB drive.
Interface34-pin Shugart/PC-compatible signal edge connector; separate 4-pin +5 V/+12 V power.
Data rate / speed125 kbit/s FM and 250 kbit/s MFM; 300 rpm standard rotation.
Jumpers / drive selectFD-55 option block supports DS0–DS3 and interface/LED options. Configure the exact DS input and ready/disk-change functions per the model's installation manual; terminate only at the bus end.
Dimensions / power146 × 41 × 203 mm (W×H×D), half-height; +5 V 0.38 A typical (0.6 A max), +12 V 0.25 A typical (0.9 A max); 4-pin power connector.
PC controller compatibilityUsable with a PC floppy controller at 250 kbit/s MFM for supported 40-track formats; configure one-sided geometry. It cannot provide a second read/write head or 360 KB double-sided geometry.

Preservation notes

Label this drive explicitly as single-sided and 40-track. Preserve any surviving format settings and do not attempt head alignment with double-sided reference media; retain raw disk images and acquisition notes separately from filesystem copies.
