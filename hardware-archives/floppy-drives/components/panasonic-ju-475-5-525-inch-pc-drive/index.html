Panasonic JU-475-5 5.25-inch PC floppy drive

A 5.25-inch half-height, double-sided HD drive in Panasonic's JU-475 series. The exact suffix matters: service settings for the JU-475-2/-4 and JU-475-5 are not interchangeable without checking the model documentation.

Documented specifications

Density / capacityDouble-sided, 80-track/96-TPI high density; 1.2 MB formatted HD and 360 KB PC DD media support, with low-density interchange subject to speed and alignment.
Interface34-pin PC-compatible signal connector and 4-pin +5 V/+12 V power.
Data rate / speed500 kbit/s HD; 250 kbit/s DD; 360 rpm HD and 300 rpm low-density mode as configured.
Jumpers / drive selectJU-475-series select and function jumpers configure drive address, motor, ready/disk-change and speed options. Set the marked DS position for the cable twist; use the JU-475-5 manual/label for exact positions.
Dimensions / power41.5 × 146 × 196.5 mm half-height; +5 V 0.44 A typical/0.5 A max, +12 V 0.26 A typical/0.8 A max; 4-pin power.
PC controller compatibilityIBM PC/AT-compatible 765-family controller configured for a 5.25-inch 1.2 MB drive. Do not presume correct 360 KB interchange without a tested alignment and controller setup.

Preservation notes

Keep the complete suffix in all collection records. Photograph the DIP/jumper area and resistor pack; capture baseline operation and test-disk identity before cleaning or adjustment. Avoid writing to original media during initial diagnostics.
