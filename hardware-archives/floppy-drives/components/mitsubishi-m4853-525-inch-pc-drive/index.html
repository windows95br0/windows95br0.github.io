Mitsubishi M4853 5.25-inch PC floppy drive

A half-height Mitsubishi 5.25-inch mechanism documented by dedicated maintenance and OEM manuals. Use the model-specific diagrams rather than assuming another Mitsubishi family member has the same wiring.

Documented specifications

Density / capacityDouble-sided 5.25-inch high-density PC mechanism; standard 1.2 MB HD format, with 360 KB DD media support dependent on alignment and configuration.
Interface34-pin PC floppy data/control connection with separate 4-pin +5 V/+12 V drive power.
Data rate / speed500 kbit/s HD PC controller mode; lower DD rates are controller/media dependent. HD operation is 360 rpm; verify DD speed behavior in the exact M4853 manual.
Jumpers / drive selectDrive-select and interface options are set at the drive's configuration block. Select the PC drive number per the OEM diagram and set termination only as specified for bus position.
Dimensions / powerHalf-height 5.25-inch chassis, approximately 146 × 41 × 203 mm; nominal +5 V and +12 V supply rails. Confirm current draw from the exact manual revision before servicing the power supply.
PC controller compatibilityFor IBM PC/AT-compatible 765-family controllers with 5.25-inch HD support; confirm the controller/BIOS supports 1.2 MB drives and select the correct drive type.

Preservation notes

Capture the label, PCB identifier, revision and jumper layout before cleaning or service. Test with expendable, known-good media first; a 1.2 MB drive may not reliably read every 360 KB disk without correct alignment and speed.
