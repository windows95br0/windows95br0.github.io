[ SCOPE NOTE ]

What else belongs here?

The catalog also flags commonly overlooked families: firmware chips and BIOS/UEFI, embedded controllers, storage controllers, risers and backplanes, docking stations, webcams, microphones, printers, scanners, KVMs, card readers, security modules, and accessibility hardware. They are included in the index notes and can receive their own records as the collection grows.

Each model record has one primary category and listing route. Related browse paths form directories rather than duplicate listings: CPU and GPU browsing stays vendor-first, while the UPS topology lists sit under their shared directory. Legacy taxonomy URLs remain available as compatibility landing pages.
