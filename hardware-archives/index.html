What else belongs here?

The catalog also flags commonly overlooked families: firmware chips and BIOS/UEFI, embedded controllers, storage controllers, risers and backplanes, docking stations, webcams, microphones, printers, scanners, KVMs, card readers, security modules, and accessibility hardware.
