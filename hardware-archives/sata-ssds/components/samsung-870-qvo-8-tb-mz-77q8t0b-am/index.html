Samsung 870 QVO 8 TB

Model-specific record for manufacturer SKU MZ-77Q8T0B/AM. See linked manufacturer documentation for rated values and revision details.

IDENTIFICATION: Confirm full SKU MZ-77Q8T0B/AM, capacity, package-region suffix and hardware revision against the physical label; do not assume same-family variants share internals.

Specifications

Manufacturer / modelSamsung 870 QVO 8 TB
Full manufacturer SKUMZ-77Q8T0B/AM
Capacity8 TB
Form factor / height2.5-inch SATA, 7 mm (check physical clearance and the manufacturer’s exact package/revision documentation).
Interface / limitSATA III / SATA 6 Gb/s signaling. Protocol overhead and negotiated host link speed limit payload throughput; M.2 SATA, mSATA and NVMe are not interchangeable connectors/protocols.
Controller, NAND, endurance & performanceSamsung specifies 4-bit V-NAND (QLC), MKX controller, sequential reads up to 560 MB/s and writes up to 530 MB/s, and 2,880 TBW for the 8 TB version. SATA 6 Gb/s is the signaling limit, not a promised payload rate.
Host compatibilityFor a PC/laptop with a 2.5-inch bay or suitable mounting and a SATA data connector plus SATA power. SATA backward compatibility depends on host implementation; check BIOS/UEFI, operating-system and boot support, connector clearance and required capacity support. TRIM/SMART requires suitable OS, driver and ATA pass-through support; some USB bridges/RAID modes do not pass commands.

Archival, health & service notes

  • Record label, serial, firmware, SMART/health snapshot, host/controller, date and source revision; a family name alone is not enough to identify a revision.
  • Advertised maxima and TBW are manufacturer ratings, not a guarantee of speed or service life in a particular workload.
  • Keep verified backups and periodic integrity checks; an SSD is not a substitute for multiple independent archival copies.
