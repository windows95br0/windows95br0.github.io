Accessibility and safety notes

Hands-free motion control can support users unable to operate a conventional mouse; adjust pointer speed, filtering and control direction to reduce effort. External switches or dwell can provide click access.

Secure the selected wearable mount and avoid pressure on the head/skin; take breaks and stop for discomfort, nausea or fatigue. Charge only as instructed and keep cable/receiver from snagging. This is not a medical-grade or emergency control.

Document observed condition separately from manufacturer specifications. Test activation and host response in a controlled, non-safety-critical setup; record accessories, host OS, driver/app and firmware versions.
