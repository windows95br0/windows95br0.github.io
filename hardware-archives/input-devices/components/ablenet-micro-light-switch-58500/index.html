Accessibility and safety notes

Extremely light activation force supports users with limited strength; the small active area requires deliberate, safe placement and testing for accidental activation.

Prevent pressure or strain on skin/joints, secure cable slack and inspect its plug/lead. Protect from moisture; clean per manufacturer instructions and do not exceed a comfortable movement range.

Document observed condition separately from manufacturer specifications. Test activation and host response in a controlled, non-safety-critical setup; record accessories, host OS, driver/app and firmware versions.
