Nintendo Joy-Con (L/R) Controllers

Detachable paired mini controllers used on Switch rail or wirelessly as separate input devices.

MODEL IDENTIFICATION: HAC-015 (L) / HAC-016 (R). Confirm the printed SKU, revision, bundle, region, and supplied accessories before repair or compatibility claims.

Model specifications

Catalog leafGame controllers
Exact model / identifierHAC-015 (L) / HAC-016 (R)
Physical mechanism and primary specificationsBluetooth; rail contacts for attached mode/charging; each controller has motion sensing, HD Rumble, and buttons; right Joy-Con includes IR motion camera/NFC capabilities as specified by Nintendo.
Compatibility and interface caveatsJoy-Con left/right are separate model IDs and are not interchangeable. Host/PC behavior and split-controller pairing depend on software and OS.
Revision identificationRecord complete product label, revision, region, and accessories. Similar family names may differ in protocol, connector, feature set, and host requirements.

Preservation and service

Preserve matching L/R pair and wrist straps; check rail locks and battery health, avoiding force on slide rails.

Record observed condition, accessories, host OS, driver/app version, and test conditions separately from vendor specifications.
