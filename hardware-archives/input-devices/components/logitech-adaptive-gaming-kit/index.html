Logitech Adaptive Gaming Kit

Set of external buttons and switches designed for Xbox Adaptive Controller switch ports.

MODEL IDENTIFICATION: Exact model / identifier: Adaptive Gaming Kit. Confirm label, package suffix, region, and revision against manufacturer documentation.

Model specifications

Catalog subcategoryAdaptive and switch-access controls
Exact model / IDAdaptive Gaming Kit
Physical mechanism and primary specificationsKit contains 12 plug-and-play switches in several button sizes and trigger styles, 3.5 mm switch leads, hook-and-loop mats, and ties. No processor or standalone host interface.
Host, interface, and driver caveats3.5 mm plugs are switch closures, not audio connectors. Function depends on port mapping in the Xbox Adaptive Controller or another documented switch interface; not a direct USB PC keyboard.

Preservation and service notes

Inventory buttons, leads, and mounting pieces together. Identify switch behavior before substitution; do not immerse housings, kink leads, or pull switches by cables.
