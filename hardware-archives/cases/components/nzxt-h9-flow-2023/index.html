NZXT H9 Flow (2023)

The 2023 H9 Flow is a wide dual-chamber mid-tower with a separate cable/drive chamber and a perforated lower intake. Its layout allows cooling hardware on the side, roof, and floor, so fit depends on the radiator/fan stacks and the graphics-card envelope, not on the nominal case dimensions alone.

MODEL SCOPE: Specifications describe the named enclosure generation, not every visually similar case or retail bundle. Published maximums are component-envelope limits; verify the exact SKU, installed fans/radiators, connectors, and manufacturer manual before purchase or assembly.

Fit notes for this layout

The Flow and Elite are separate bundles: supplied fan count, RGB controller and included accessories are not interchangeable. NZXT’s 2023-series specifications are the source of truth for this record, not the later H9 Flow RGB family.

  • A bottom radiator/fan stack sits below the GPU; check card thickness, bottom clearance and the intake path before populating both positions.
  • The vertical side radiator position can be assembled as intake or exhaust, but its orientation and tubing route should be checked against GPU length and service access.
  • Use the 2023 H9 Flow manual and exact SKU for fan/controller contents; later Flow RGB variants are distinct product configurations.
