[ IDENTIFIED ENCLOSURE // ML03 ]

SilverStone Milo ML03

A 15.7 L low-profile media chassis for Micro-ATX builds; its 70 mm CPU-cooler ceiling and 279 mm graphics-card limit govern component selection.

MODEL / FIT NOTE: The ML03 is the wide, low-profile member of the Milo HTPC line; distinguish it from the similarly sized ML04 by its 440 mm width and four expansion slots.

Mechanical specifications and fit

Model and revisionSilverStone Milo ML03 (chassis model ML03).
Dimensions / volume440 × 105 × 340 mm (W × H × D); approximately 15.7 L.
Motherboard / slotsMicro-ATX or Mini-ITX motherboard; four expansion slots.
Power supplyStandard ATX/PS2 PSU, up to 140 mm long.
Graphics-card clearanceUp to 279 mm long; full-height dual-slot cards, subject to adjacent cabling and slot geometry.
CPU-cooler clearanceCPU cooler up to 70 mm high.
Radiator clearanceNo dedicated radiator mount specified; use the chassis fan positions for air cooling.
Drive clearance / baysOne slim optical drive; up to two 3.5-inch and one 2.5-inch internal drives (some mounts are shared/configurable).

Compatibility notes

Published maxima are envelopes, not a guarantee that every maximum fits simultaneously. Measure the selected card, PSU body and cable exits, CPU cooler and radiator/fan stack together; confirm drive-cage position and panel closure before purchase or assembly.

  • Install only the motherboard standoffs matching the supported board layout and verify no unused standoff touches the PCB.
  • Check power-plug bend radius, graphics-card thickness and intake clearance against the exact panel and drive-bracket configuration.
  • Use the cited manufacturer manual for installation sequence and revision-specific bracket placement; do not infer part interchangeability from a shared product family.
