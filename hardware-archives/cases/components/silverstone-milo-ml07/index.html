[ IDENTIFIED ENCLOSURE // ML07 ]

SilverStone Milo ML07

A slim 14 L Mini-ITX chassis with a horizontal/vertical layout, long dual-slot graphics support and shallow CPU-cooler clearance.

MODEL / FIT NOTE: The layout is closely related to some Raven slim chassis but ML07 is its own model. Radiator, long GPU and optional 3.5-inch storage clearances are not additive.

Mechanical specifications and fit

Model and revisionSilverStone Milo ML07 (chassis model ML07).
Dimensions / volume382 × 105 × 350 mm (W × H × D); approximately 14.0 L.
Motherboard / slotsMini-ITX motherboard; two expansion slots.
Power supplySFX or SFX-L power supply (SFX-L length reduces cable and GPU working room).
Graphics-card clearanceUp to 330 mm long, dual-slot card; verify card width and power-connector bend against the panel.
CPU-cooler clearanceCPU cooler up to 83 mm high.
Radiator clearanceSupports a side-mounted radiator up to 240 mm in the dedicated side position; the radiator/fan stack shares space with the graphics-card and storage envelope.
Drive clearance / baysThree 2.5-inch mounts; optional 3.5-inch mounting is conditional on removing or relocating other brackets.

Compatibility notes

Published maxima are envelopes, not a guarantee that every maximum fits simultaneously. Measure the selected card, PSU body and cable exits, CPU cooler and radiator/fan stack together; confirm drive-cage position and panel closure before purchase or assembly.

  • Install only the motherboard standoffs matching the supported board layout and verify no unused standoff touches the PCB.
  • Check power-plug bend radius, graphics-card thickness and intake clearance against the exact panel and drive-bracket configuration.
  • Use the cited manufacturer manual for installation sequence and revision-specific bracket placement; do not infer part interchangeability from a shared product family.
