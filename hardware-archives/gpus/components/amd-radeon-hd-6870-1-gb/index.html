AMD Radeon HD 6870 1 GB

Barts XT, 2010. This record identifies the listed retail model/configuration; board-partner variations are called out separately.

MODEL / VARIANT: 1 GB GDDR5 Radeon HD 6870 (Barts XT), not HD 6850 or 2 GB/overclocked partner boards.

Recorded specifications

Cataloged productAMD Radeon HD 6870 1 GB
GPU / architecture / launchBarts XT; 2010.
Core & memoryBarts XT GPU; 1,120 stream processors; 1 GB GDDR5 on a 256-bit bus; reference clocks of 900 MHz GPU and 1,050 MHz memory (4.2 GT/s effective).
Host interface & powerPCI Express 2.1 x16; reference board uses two 6-pin PCIe auxiliary-power connectors. 151 W maximum board-power class; use a PSU with both 6-pin leads and adequate 12 V capacity.
Typical reference outputsReference panel typically provides dual DVI, HDMI and two mini-DisplayPort outputs; inspect the actual card before sourcing adapters.
Board dimensionsReference board length is approximately 255 mm, with a two-slot cooler; partner/OEM revisions can differ. Verify the exact board drawing and slot clearance before installation.
Variant boundary1 GB GDDR5 Radeon HD 6870 (Barts XT), not HD 6850 or 2 GB/overclocked partner boards.

Compatibility, service & archival notes

Check PCIe slot/firmware support and both auxiliary power connectors. Confirm cooler thickness/length and airflow in the chassis. Older operating systems may need a legacy Catalyst/Crimson package; choose a driver supporting the card device ID and OS. Display output combinations and adapters vary by board.

  • Check the exact slot keying/signaling, board dimensions, auxiliary-power pinout, PSU guidance, airflow and display outputs for the specimen; connector appearance alone does not establish compatibility.
  • Install a driver supporting the recorded device ID and target OS. Preserve the original driver and firmware versions before making changes.
  • Record board partner, PCB and cooler revision, device/subsystem IDs, BIOS, adapter set and physical condition. Do not power a card with damage, corrosion, obstructed cooling or suspected shorts.
