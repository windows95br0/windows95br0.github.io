MGA-G400 MAX graphics processor, 1999. This entry describes one identifiable model or reference configuration; related products and OEM variants are distinguished below.

Compatibility, service & archival notes

Reference MAX clocks do not identify the connector panel; distinguish G400 MAX from G400 by label and board markings.

Record PCB/BIOS and Matrox driver version; retain adapters. Inspect connectors and fanless heatsink; avoid stressing SGRAM packages when cleaning.

  • Check signaling, slot keying, lane width, power pinout, board length, case clearance, airflow, and PSU requirements for the exact item.
  • Use drivers for the device ID and target OS; preserve installer/firmware copies and record their source before updates.
  • Do not power hardware with corrosion, cracks, damaged connectors, obstructed cooling, or suspected shorts; document condition before bench testing.
