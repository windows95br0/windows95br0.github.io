AMD FirePro W4300 4 GB

A 2015 Bonaire-based professional adapter that brought 4 GB of memory and four outputs to compact workstation systems.

Recorded specifications

GPUBonaire-class GCN; 768 stream processors.
Memory4 GB GDDR5, 128-bit memory interface.
Interface & powerPCI Express 3.0 x16; 50 W board-power class; no auxiliary PCIe power connector.
OutputsFour Mini DisplayPort 1.2 connectors with AMD multi-display support, subject to driver, display and adapter capability.
Physical fitSingle-slot, half-length low-profile design class. Match the card's actual bracket and shroud dimensions to the chassis, and confirm adjacent-slot/airflow clearance.

Workstation & driver compatibility

Suitable for compact professional desktops that need several displays or modest CAD acceleration. Confirm the workstation provides PCIe x16 clearance and a compatible low-profile bracket.

  • Use AMD's archived FirePro professional driver only when its release notes list W4300 and the exact OS. App certification is version-specific and does not imply current software support.
  • Check the workstation OEM's validated GPU list, BIOS support, cooling and adapter compatibility; do not assume all MiniDP-to-DVI/HDMI adapters are interchangeable.
  • Retain driver and firmware versions with the card's part/subsystem ID; inspect its compact cooler and ports before service.
