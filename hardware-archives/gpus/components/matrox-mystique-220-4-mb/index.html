Matrox Mystique 220 4 MB PCI

MGA-1164SG Mystique 220, 1997. This entry identifies one model or reference configuration; related products and OEM variants are distinguished below.

MODEL / VARIANT: Mystique 220 is distinct from original Mystique and Mystique 220 8 MB/DualHead variants. Early 3D feature support and texture filtering are limited relative to later accelerators.

Compatibility, service & archival notes

A 2D desktop card with early 3D capabilities, not the later G200. Memory/output options vary by board maker.

Record PCB and chip markings; preserve Matrox driver/CD. Inspect SGRAM, VGA solder joints, and edge contacts; original 3D API behavior depends on period driver and game.

  • Check signaling, slot keying, lane width, power pinout, board/module dimensions, case clearance, airflow and PSU requirements for the exact specimen.
  • Use drivers supporting its device ID and target OS. Preserve original installer and firmware copies, and record their versions before changes.
  • Do not energize boards with corrosion, cracks, damaged connectors, obstructed cooling or suspected shorts; document condition before bench testing.
