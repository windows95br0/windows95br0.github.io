DDR2-800 200-pin SO-DIMM

DDR2 SO-DIMM module standard. This record identifies a distinct product or standards-defined module class; specifications not verified for this exact identity are deliberately left open.

IDENTITY: DDR2-800 200-pin SO-DIMM is cataloged as ddr2 so-dimm module standard. Standards entries describe a module class; named manufacturer entries describe one SKU. Do not treat them as interchangeable records.

Recorded specifications

Catalog identityDDR2-800 200-pin SO-DIMM
Record typeDDR2 SO-DIMM module standard
Verified specifications200-pin small-outline DIMM; DDR2-800 (400 MHz clock, 800 MT/s effective transfer class); PC2-6400 bandwidth class. Standard voltage is 1.8 V; exact capacity, ranks, and timings vary by module.
CompatibilityFits only DDR2 SO-DIMM slots; do not confuse with 200-pin DDR SO-DIMM, which has a different keying and signaling. Laptop chipset, BIOS, supported density, and maximum capacity must be checked.
Unverified/variable detailsOnly values supported by the cited standard or manufacturer source are stated. Confirm exact part number/revision, SPD, ranks, timings, and voltage on the specimen when not specified above.

Platform compatibility and service

  • Fits only DDR2 SO-DIMM slots; do not confuse with 200-pin DDR SO-DIMM, which has a different keying and signaling. Laptop chipset, BIOS, supported density, and maximum capacity must be checked.
  • Before installation, record the full label, manufacturer, part number, revision/date code, contact condition, chip markings, and SPD contents where present.
  • Use ESD precautions, handle the module by its edges, and never force it into a slot with different keying. Power down and follow the host’s service manual.
  • For server/ECC modules, confirm CPU support, buffering type, channel population, supported ranks/densities, and firmware behavior; physical fit is not proof of ECC operation.

Archival and service notes

A form-factor and speed-class record, not a single manufacturer SKU. Record the module label and device/chip organization; aging laptops may accept only particular ranks or densities. Photograph both sides before cleaning; retain the host manual, SPD dump, test host, BIOS revision, and memory-test result with the catalog record.
