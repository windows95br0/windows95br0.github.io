Compatibility and installation

Mount only with the bracket and hardware supplied for this Pure Rock 2 FX revision. Connect the fan to a 4-pin PWM header and its 5 V addressable-lighting lead only to a matching 3-pin 5 V ARGB header; never connect ARGB to a 12 V RGB header.

  • Disconnect system power before installation or rewiring, and follow the exact model manual.
  • After assembly, confirm fan/pump operation and monitor temperatures before sustained workload.
