Cooler Master CryoFuze 7 2 g

2 g syringe of CryoFuze 7 thermal paste; the maker describes it as electrically insulating and non-corrosive.

IDENTIFICATION: CryoFuze 7 is distinct from CryoFuze 5 and other CryoFuze formulations. Check the product name and net weight printed on the syringe before service.

Application & compatibility cautions

  • Apply as a thin interface layer only on surfaces for which the CPU/GPU and cooler instructions specify thermal paste.
  • Do not treat paste as a replacement for a pad or putty used to bridge a component-height gap; match OEM pad placement and thickness.
  • Power down before service, avoid socket contacts and connectors, and clean/reapply after separating the cooler from the processor.
