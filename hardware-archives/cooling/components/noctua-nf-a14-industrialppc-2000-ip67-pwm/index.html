Noctua NF-A14 industrialPPC-2000 IP67 PWM

Sealed IP67 140 mm industrial PWM fan, distinct from the non-sealed NF-A14 industrialPPC-2000 PWM model.

Recorded specifications

Exact partNF-A14 industrialPPC-2000 IP67 PWM (manufacturer model designation)
Frame / thickness140 × 140 × 25 mm
Speed / airflow / pressure / noise500–2000 RPM; up to 182.5 m³/h; 4.18 mm H₂O maximum static pressure; 31.5 dB(A).
BearingSSO2 self-stabilizing oil-pressure bearing
Electrical / control12 V DC; 0.13 A maximum input current; 4-pin PWM connector. IP67 protection is a property of the fan construction, not the PC header.
Case / radiator compatibilityStandard 140 mm mounting pattern and 25 mm frame depth; usable in compatible case or radiator locations. Check screw length, airflow orientation and controller current. Sealing does not make the motherboard or wiring waterproof.

Installation notes

This sealed model is intended for environments where ingress protection is useful. Keep the 4-pin PWM connector and all host connections within their own environmental ratings.
