Intel Core i5-1035G1

Ice Lake (10th-generation Core), 2019. 4 cores / 8 threads; maximum turbo up to 3.60 GHz; 6 MB Intel Smart Cache; 15 W TDP; configurable 13–25 W; FCBGA1526, soldered; Dual-channel DDR4-3200 or LPDDR4x-3733; platform wiring determines supported capacity. Ice Lake moved this four-core mobile Core i5 to Intel 10 nm and integrated Gen11 graphics; G1 denotes its 32-EU graphics tier.

MODEL IDENTIFICATION: Intel Core i5-1035G1. Confirm the complete model suffix and OEM board documentation; this is a soldered mobile package, not a universal replacement part.

Compatibility and service notes

  • Ice Lake-U notebooks with a compatible FCBGA1526 board, firmware and thermal design. The package is not a socketed upgrade.
  • Dual-channel DDR4-3200 or LPDDR4x-3733; platform wiring determines supported capacity Memory speed, channel wiring and any memory-on-package configuration must match the system-board design.
  • Check the laptop manufacturer's exact model-level CPU list, board revision, BIOS/firmware, thermal assembly and power profile. Package name alone does not guarantee electrical, firmware or mechanical compatibility.
  • PCI Express capabilities do not promise a particular connector, storage slot or external port; those are implemented by the notebook platform.

Service and archival notes

Ice Lake moved this four-core mobile Core i5 to Intel 10 nm and integrated Gen11 graphics; G1 denotes its 32-EU graphics tier. Record the complete processor marking, system SKU, board revision, BIOS, installed memory topology and cooling/power configuration with any service or test observation.
