Intel Xeon W-2123

Skylake-W (Xeon W-2100 series), 2017. Exact model: Intel Xeon W-2123. 4 cores/8 threads; 3.60 GHz base / 3.90 GHz maximum turbo; 8.25 MB cache; 120 W TDP; LGA2066.

Compatibility and service

  • Use an LGA2066 C422 workstation board with explicit Xeon W-2123 support and the required BIOS; chipset/socket match alone is insufficient.
  • Populate four memory channels with board-qualified DDR4-2666 ECC UDIMMs or other specifically validated memory; capacity is platform dependent.
  • Provide cooling and power delivery rated for the 120 W processor and verify cooler clearance/mounting.
  • No integrated graphics; provide a discrete GPU or other display adapter.
