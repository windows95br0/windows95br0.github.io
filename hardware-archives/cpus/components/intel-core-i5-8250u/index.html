Intel Core i5-8250U

Kaby Lake Refresh (8th-generation Core U-series), 2017. 4 cores / 8 threads; maximum turbo up to 3.40 GHz; 6 MB Intel Smart Cache; 15 W TDP; configurable 10–25 W; FCBGA1356, soldered; Dual-channel DDR4-2400 or LPDDR3-2133; board design sets capacity and channel population. A four-core Kaby Lake Refresh part that brought mainstream Core i5 ultrabooks to eight threads; the U suffix identifies its low-power mobile role.

MODEL IDENTIFICATION: Intel Core i5-8250U. Confirm the complete model suffix and OEM board documentation; this is a soldered mobile package, not a universal replacement part.
