Intel 8085A

Intel 8085 NMOS model introduced 1976. 8-bit CPU, 16-bit address, 3 MHz, 40-pin DIP, single +5 V supply, integrated clock and serial I/O.

Compatibility and variants

  • 8085 bus/timing; not 8080A pin-compatible.
  • 8085AH is later HMOS; check suffix/electrical limits.
  • Check system-vendor CPU support, board revision, firmware minimum, package, memory, cooling and power before installation; similar socket naming does not prove compatibility.
