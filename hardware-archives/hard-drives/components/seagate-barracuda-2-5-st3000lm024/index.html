Seagate BarraCuda ST3000LM024

3 TB, 2.5-inch SATA drive with a 15 mm z-height. This thick, high-capacity mobile-class drive does not fit many standard notebook bays.

IDENTIFICATION: This page covers the ST3000LM024 standard model. Verify the exact label; bridge/enclosure products can use different firmware or included interfaces.

Sector / recording format512e logical sector presentation; shingled magnetic recording (SMR). Confirm logical/physical sector reporting on the individual unit.

Compatibility, noise, shock and reliability

  • Requires a SATA host, 5 V supply and 15 mm drive clearance; confirm the enclosure actually supports this z-height and can dissipate heat. A common 7/9.5 mm laptop bay is too thin.
  • Manufacturer documentation gives typical noise/sound power around 2.4 bel idle / 2.6 bel operating and shock limits of 300 G operating (2 ms) / 650 G non-operating (1 ms).
  • SMR can exhibit lower sustained-write performance after cache exhaustion and different rebuild behavior than CMR. Do not assume suitability for RAID, write-intensive workloads or capacity-limited bridges.
  • 600,000-hour MTBF and load/unload/error-rate values are population-level specifications, not expected individual life. Abnormal clicking or read errors warrant stopping writes and preserving data.
