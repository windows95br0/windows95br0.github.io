Compatibility, noise, shock and reliability

  • Check SATA host support, 9.5 mm mounting clearance and 5 V power. Some older systems impose BIOS capacity or boot constraints even when the physical connection fits.
  • The manufacturer family sheet specifies mode-dependent idle/seek noise (about 2.3 bel idle and 2.5–3.0 bel seeking) and shock limits of 350 G operating (2 ms) / 900 G non-operating (1 ms).
  • Shock values describe controlled tests and do not endorse drops or handling while spinning. Keep backups; cycle-life and error-rate ratings are population figures, not a remaining-life guarantee.
  • Check reported logical sectors before legacy OS installation, cloning or partitioning; preserve the original drive read-only if data recovery is the purpose.
