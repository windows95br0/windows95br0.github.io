Seagate ST-412

10 MB full-height MFM hard disk, early 1980s. This record is limited to the exact model and documented variant; related model numbers, revisions and capacities can have different interfaces and specifications.

IDENTIFICATION: Match the complete label, suffix, firmware and drive inquiry with the cited model documentation. Do not infer sector format or recording method from product family alone.

Verified model specifications

Dedicated catalog subcategoryMFM and early magnetic drives

Product generation10 MB full-height MFM hard disk, early 1980s
Form factor5.25-inch full-height class; this classification is not a dimensional drawing.
InterfaceST-506/ST-412-compatible MFM; separate data and control cables to a compatible controller.
Capacity, performance, sector / recording detailsThe ST-412 OEM manual specifies a 10 MB formatted class drive and model-specific geometry. Preserve the manual’s exact geometry and controller formatting; formatted capacity depends on controller and format.
CompatibilityNeeds an MFM/ST-506 controller with matching geometry, correct drive select/termination, and separate control/data cabling. Not compatible with IDE or SCSI hosts directly.

Handling, service & preservation

  • Record the original jumper and termination positions, cable routing, controller geometry, and power supply before service. Do not interchange cable position based on modern connector assumptions.
  • Photograph the full label, board marks, connectors, jumpers/carrier, and date/firmware codes before service; retain the exact model suffix with collection metadata.
  • Never open a hard drive outside a qualified clean environment. Stop repeated power-on attempts if the unit clicks, drops offline, or develops read errors.
  • Preserve source data by making independent copies and validating checksums; SMART/self-tests cannot guarantee reliability. Distinguish measured condition from manufacturer specifications.
