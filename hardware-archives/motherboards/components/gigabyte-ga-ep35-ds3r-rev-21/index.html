GIGABYTE GA-EP35-DS3R (rev. 2.1)

Intel P35 / LGA775 ATX board, 2008. Exact rev. 2.1; DDR2, single graphics slot, SATA controllers, CPU list and BIOS. This record identifies one exact SKU/revision; verify the silkscreened board marking before firmware or component selection.

IDENTIFICATION: This entry is for GA-EP35-DS3R, PCB revision 2.1. Similar family names, chipset stepping and board revisions may use incompatible BIOS, layouts or connectors.

Specifications & compatibility

Exact board / PCB revisionGA-EP35-DS3R — PCB revision 2.1
Platform / socket / chipset2008 Intel desktop platform; LGA775; Intel P35 Express + ICH9R
CPU compatibilityIntel Core 2 Quad/Core 2 Duo, Pentium and Celeron LGA775 CPUs listed for GA-EP35-DS3R rev. 2.1 only; confirm FSB, stepping and minimum BIOS.
Form factor / dimensionsATX, 305 × 244 mm
MemoryFour DDR2 DIMMs, up to 8 GB dual-channel non-ECC unbuffered DDR2; higher frequencies are OC modes.
ExpansionOne PCIe x16 graphics slot, three PCIe x1 and three legacy PCI slots.
StorageSix Intel ICH9R SATA 3 Gb/s and two GIGABYTE SATA2 ports; one IDE/PATA and one floppy connector. Check manual for controller RAID modes and boot limits.
External I/ORear PS/2, USB 2.0, Gigabit Ethernet, 8-channel HD audio and digital audio; internal USB/audio headers support case front I/O.
BIOS / firmware supportDualBIOS and Q-Flash. Match exact GA-EP35-DS3R rev. 2.1 BIOS image; GIGABYTE’s CPU support list identifies minimum BIOS for each supported processor.
PSU / case compatibilityStandard ATX case with 305 × 244 mm motherboard clearance and matching standoffs. Requires a standard 24-pin ATX main connector plus 8-pin CPU 12 V input.

Installation and service notes

Confirm the exact CPU, minimum BIOS, DIMM type/capacity and supported slot/port mapping in the board-specific vendor support documents before purchase or installation. Socket fit is not proof of processor support; similarly named board firmware must not be cross-flashed.

Use ESD-safe handling, inspect the board for socket damage, leakage and corrosion, and keep loose screws or conductive debris away from the PCB. Check the case standoffs, PSU leads and cooler clearance against the manual before first power-on.
