ASUS P5K-E/WiFi-AP (PCB Rev. 1.02G)

Intel P35 / LGA775 ATX platform, 2007. PCB revision 1.02G; DDR2, Wi-Fi-AP package, CPU list and BIOS requirements are model-specific. This record identifies one exact SKU/revision; verify the silkscreened board marking before firmware or component selection.

IDENTIFICATION: This entry is for P5K-E/WiFi-AP, PCB revision 1.02G. Similar family names, chipset stepping and board revisions may use incompatible BIOS, layouts or connectors.

Specifications & compatibility

Exact board / PCB revisionP5K-E/WiFi-AP — PCB revision 1.02G
Platform / socket / chipset2007 Intel desktop platform; LGA775; Intel P35 Express + ICH9R
CPU compatibilityIntel Core 2 Quad/Duo, Pentium Dual-Core and Celeron LGA775 CPUs only where the ASUS P5K-E CPU support table lists the exact stepping and minimum BIOS. 45 nm CPU support is not implied by socket fit alone.
Form factor / dimensionsATX, 305 × 244 mm
MemoryFour DDR2 DIMM slots, up to 8 GB dual-channel non-ECC unbuffered DDR2; faster profiles are overclock-dependent.
ExpansionTwo PCIe 2.0 x16-length slots (one x16 and one x4 electrical), two PCIe x1 and three legacy PCI slots.
StorageSix Intel ICH9R SATA 3 Gb/s ports, one PATA connector, one floppy connector and board-level eSATA via the secondary controller.
External I/ORear PS/2, USB 2.0, IEEE 1394, Gigabit Ethernet, analog audio and digital S/PDIF; Wi-Fi-AP package adds 802.11b/g wireless accessory support.
BIOS / firmware supportASUS CrashFree BIOS 2 and EZ Flash 2; CPU support is model- and minimum-BIOS-specific. Confirm the P5K-E CPU table and installed BIOS before fitting a processor; never use firmware for a similar P5K variant.
PSU / case compatibilityATX case with 305 × 244 mm board clearance, matching standoffs and expansion openings. Use a standards-compliant ATX PSU with 24-pin EATXPWR and 8-pin ATX12V connections; do not force a PCIe 8-pin plug into the CPU power socket.

Installation and service notes

Confirm the exact CPU, minimum BIOS, DIMM type/capacity and supported slot/port mapping in the board-specific vendor support documents before purchase or installation. Socket fit is not proof of processor support; similarly named board firmware must not be cross-flashed.

Use ESD-safe handling, inspect the board for socket damage, leakage and corrosion, and keep loose screws or conductive debris away from the PCB. Check the case standoffs, PSU leads and cooler clearance against the manual before first power-on.
