Intel Desktop Board D815EGEW

Intel microATX board for Socket 370 Pentium III/Celeron processors. Its 815EG chipset supplies integrated video; the D815EGEW layout is not the same as other 815E/EP board variants.

Exact identity and revision: Intel Desktop Board D815EGEW; Intel Product Guide revision -001 and BIOS family EW81510A.86A. Confirm the board's AA number and BIOS ID before CPU selection or firmware recovery.

Specifications

CPU / socket / chipsetSocket 370 Intel Pentium III (100/133 MHz FSB) and Celeron (66/100 MHz FSB), subject to the support list; Intel 815EG integrated-graphics chipset.
Form factor / caseMicroATX, 9.6 x 7.65 in.; microATX/ATX case only after checking standoffs, I/O shield, and drive-bay clearance.
MemoryTwo 168-pin PC100/PC133 SDRAM DIMM sockets; capacity/module density must match the Intel guide and board revision.
ExpansionFour PCI slots; no AGP connector on this board.
StorageTwo IDE channels supporting ATA/100 and one floppy interface.
Rear I/OVGA, PS/2 keyboard/mouse, serial, parallel, two USB, and audio; Intel 10/100 LAN is an option on some assemblies.
BIOS / PSUIntel BIOS family EW81510A.86A. Standard ATX 20-pin power input; check the board's actual header, supply wiring, and rail capacity before use.

Compatibility and revision notes

The integrated video is part of the 815EG platform. Since this model has no AGP slot, do not plan an AGP upgrade; verify any PCI graphics card instead. Use the D815EGEW manual and BIOS family, not firmware for D815EEA/EPFV. Confirm microATX mounting and optional LAN presence on the physical board.
