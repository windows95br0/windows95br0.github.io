MSI K9N Neo V3

AMD Socket AM2 nForce 560 desktop platform, 2007. This record documents the named model and its documented variants; confirm the PCB revision, submodel and firmware on the physical unit.

Compatibility and revision notes

Socket AM2 CPUs use DDR2; AM2+ processor compatibility depends on CPU stepping and BIOS. This board has one x16 graphics slot and is not an SLI board.

Socket, connector shape and family branding alone do not establish compatibility. Verify the exact processor list, minimum BIOS, memory population table, power input, dimensions, and shared-lane behavior in the cited manual before installation.

Preservation and repair

Inspect AM2 socket pins, capacitors near the CPU power stage and the four SATA connectors. Save boot/drive mode and fan settings before CMOS service; verify cooler retention and case airflow.

For archival preservation, photograph the PCB, labels, connectors and included accessories before cleaning. Record serial/date codes, firmware versions, known faults, prior repairs and test conditions; use ESD-safe handling and do not power a board with loose conductive hardware beneath it.
