Storage

1,072 storage records, shelved by media technology rather than by connector.

Storage is shelved here by what the media physically is — flash, platter, optical, magnetic — because that is the one split that stays true. How a drive connects is a property of the drive, so SATA, SAS and NVMe solid-state drives sit together and you filter by interface instead.

Solid-state drives (291)

Flash storage, whatever it plugs into.

  • NVMe drives (148 records)
  • SATA solid-state drives (133 records)
  • SAS solid-state drives (10 records)

INTERFACES SEEN HERE: M.2, NVMe, PCI, PCIe 3.0, PCIe 4.0, PCIe 5.0, SAS, SATA, U.2, USB, mSATA
