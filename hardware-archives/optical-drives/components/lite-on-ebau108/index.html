Lite-On eBAU108

Compact external USB 2.0 DVD writer. The eBAU108 supports DVD-RAM in addition to recordable DVD/CD media; do not assume adjacent Lite-On model capabilities.

Use, compatibility & preservation

  • External slim USB 2.0, bus-powered; use a direct USB connection when startup or sustained operation is unstable through a hub.
  • Lite-On documentation associates the drive with Windows and Mac hosts; supplied burning software is OS-version dependent. The cited support page is the authority for any eBAU108 firmware/software downloads; no specific firmware version is claimed here.
  • eBAU108 is the exact model identifier; do not substitute the specifications or firmware of eBAU108-xx regional bundles without checking their labels.
  • Record drive model and reported firmware, host OS, USB controller, application version, disc format, media brand and any read or verification errors when imaging.
  • Keep checksummed image copies and logs; store discs in cases away from heat, moisture and direct sunlight. Avoid adhesive labels, abrasives and undocumented solvents.

Firmware support is model-specific: do not cross-flash a related product family or infer an update from an adjacent suffix.
