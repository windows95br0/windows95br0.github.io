Compatibility, installation & service

  • Samsung 9100 PRO requires a host with PCIe 5.0 x4 capability to approach rated throughput; on Gen4 or narrower links it negotiates lower. Check exact model suffix and firmware. UEFI NVMe boot, CPU lane availability, and slot generation are platform-specific.
  • Use the motherboard manual to identify a true PCIe 5.0 x4 M.2 slot and any CPU/slot sharing. Confirm board M.2 thermal-pad and cover clearance; do not fit the bare MPN under a heatsink that presses unevenly. Track firmware and health, back up before updates, and ensure active system airflow for long writes. Verify bootability with the exact board/BIOS, not just OS data-drive enumeration.
  • Do not hot-plug an M.2 module unless the server/platform manual explicitly documents hot-plug support. Shut down cleanly, disconnect standby power when instructed, and use electrostatic-discharge precautions during handling.
  • Before replacing a boot drive, verify a recoverable backup, OS recovery media, encryption keys, firmware settings and the target system’s boot mode; physical fit does not establish compatibility.
