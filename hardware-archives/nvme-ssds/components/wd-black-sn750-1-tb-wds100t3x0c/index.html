Compatibility, installation & service

  • Check the exact WDS100T3X0C suffix against the label; WDS100T3XHC is the distinct factory-heatsink family code. M.2 2280 shape does not imply NVMe support in a given socket. Boot support and lane sharing are board/UEFI-specific; PCIe 4/5 hosts may run this drive at Gen3 link speed.
  • Read the host manual for M-key PCIe/NVMe signaling, 2280 mounting, bootable NVMe support, and any SATA-port or PCIe-lane sharing. Use either the board heatsink or compatible factory heatsink SKU, never force the cover against a tall module. Monitor SMART temperature under sustained writes; throttling depends on airflow and host cooling. Verify firmware and use WD Dashboard only where the specific drive remains supported; back up before updates or service.
  • Do not hot-plug an M.2 module unless the server/platform manual explicitly documents hot-plug support. Shut down cleanly, disconnect standby power when instructed, and use electrostatic-discharge precautions during handling.
  • Before replacing a boot drive, verify a recoverable backup, OS recovery media, encryption keys, firmware settings and the target system’s boot mode; physical fit does not establish compatibility.
