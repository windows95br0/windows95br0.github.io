Compatibility, installation & service

  • The 2 TB bare module is not interchangeable from a cooling perspective with MP700 PRO variants equipped with an active fan or liquid cold plate. Check the full SKU, host PCIe 5.0 x4 support, and thermal clearance. OS boot capability depends on motherboard firmware/UEFI and NVMe implementation.
  • Follow the exact CORSAIR install guide and motherboard service manual for the selected cooling version, M.2 standoff, thermal interface, and airflow. Do not remove/modify factory cooling on variants that include it; avoid trapping a bare Gen5 SSD beneath an incompatible board cover. Confirm slot bandwidth/lane sharing and boot support, back up before firmware service, and monitor temperature in sustained workloads.
  • Do not hot-plug an M.2 module unless the server/platform manual explicitly documents hot-plug support. Shut down cleanly, disconnect standby power when instructed, and use electrostatic-discharge precautions during handling.
  • Before replacing a boot drive, verify a recoverable backup, OS recovery media, encryption keys, firmware settings and the target system’s boot mode; physical fit does not establish compatibility.
