Compatibility, installation & service

  • Read both motherboard and ADATA installation instructions before assembly; check the actual heatsink’s clearance, mounting and thermal-pad arrangement. Do not force a board shield over the finned cooler or remove it unless ADATA’s instructions allow. Confirm M.2 2280 Gen4 x4 NVMe wiring and boot support; retain airflow and monitor temperature under sustained transfer. Back up before firmware/service.
  • Do not assume the drive fits under every motherboard M.2 shield or laptop cover. The 2 TB specific rating is not interchangeable with another capacity. Verify suffix ALEG-960M-2TCS and included thermal hardware.
  • Confirm host boot mode, OS NVMe support, CPU lane generation, socket length/keying, power and controller/backplane qualification from the system manual; physical fit alone does not establish compatibility.
  • Before drive replacement, check backup integrity, encryption keys, firmware settings, recovery media and array rebuild plans. Use electrostatic-discharge precautions and never hot-remove unless the host documentation explicitly supports it.
