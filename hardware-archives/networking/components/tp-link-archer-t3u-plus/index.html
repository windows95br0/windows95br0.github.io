TP-Link Archer T3U Plus AC1300 High-Gain USB Wi-Fi Adapter

Dual-band Wi-Fi 5 USB 3.0 adapter with one adjustable high-gain external antenna. This record documents the named model and its labeled revision scope; it is not a claim that similarly named variants share identical drivers or radio limits.
