TP-Link Archer T4U Plus AC1300 Dual-Antenna USB Wi-Fi Adapter

Dual-band Wi-Fi 5 USB 3.0 adapter with two adjustable high-gain external antennas. This record documents the named model and its labeled revision scope; it is not a claim that similarly named variants share identical drivers or radio limits.

IDENTIFICATION: Match the full product name, model label and hardware revision with its manufacturer page before choosing a driver. Regional channel and OS support can vary.

Dedicated wireless specifications

Cataloged productTP-Link Archer T4U Plus AC1300 Dual-Antenna USB Wi-Fi Adapter
Wi-Fi generation / standardsWi-Fi 5 (IEEE 802.11ac), backward-compatible with 802.11a/b/g/n.
Radio bands, channels & width2.4 GHz and 5 GHz. 2.4 GHz supports 20/40 MHz and the 5 GHz radio supports up to 80 MHz; typical U.S. channel examples are 1–11 and 36–165, constrained by local rules and DFS.
Spatial streams / nominal link rates2×2-class AC1300: up to 400 Mb/s at 2.4 GHz and 867 Mb/s at 5 GHz; rates are nominal PHY figures and require compatible router settings.
Host interfaceUSB 3.0 Type-A, backward-compatible with earlier USB ports at reduced capacity.
Antenna arrangementTwo adjustable external high-gain dual-band antennas.
Operating-system compatibilityTP-Link offers revision-specific drivers; listed support includes Windows 11/10/8.1/8/7 and macOS versions for selected hardware revisions. Verify the revision-specific download page for exact OS build support.
BluetoothNo Bluetooth function.
Regulatory channel noteDual-band only. Usable 5 GHz channels depend on region, DFS availability and the connected access point.

Model-specific installation notes

Do not confuse this Plus model with Archer T4U V3: the Plus version has two external antennas and a different housing/interface configuration. Confirm the label and hardware revision.

2 external antennas are shown in this model’s source documentation. Install only with the correct host connector and driver package; antenna placement, local regulation and access-point capabilities affect operation.
