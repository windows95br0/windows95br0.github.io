Intel Wi-Fi 6E AX411 M.2 CNVio2 Module

AX411 is a 2×2 Wi-Fi 6E CNVio2 M.2 module with platform-dependent Double Connect features.

IDENTIFICATION: Match the full model, hardware revision, regulatory region and radio-module label before ordering replacements or selecting drivers. Values are manufacturer specifications, not a guaranteed measurement.

Dedicated wireless specifications

Cataloged productIntel Wi-Fi 6E AX411 M.2 CNVio2 Module
Chipset / radio moduleIntel AX411 CNVio2 connectivity module; the radio and host platform share functionality over Intel CNVio2.
Wi-Fi generation / standardsWi-Fi 6E (802.11ax); 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz generation support, with backward compatibility to earlier Wi-Fi standards.
Radio bands, channels & width2.4 GHz, 5 GHz and 6 GHz; up to 160 MHz channel width subject to AP, driver and regulatory domain.
Spatial streams / nominal link rates2×2 TX/RX. Intel Double Connect Technology is a platform feature, not a promise that every AX411 system can use two independent links.
Host interface / form factor / keyingM.2 2230 Key E / CNVio2 module. This proprietary host interface is distinct from a standard PCIe/USB radio module.
Antenna connectorsTwo miniature MHF4-style coax antenna sockets; inspect MAIN/AUX markings and the module revision before connecting.
BluetoothBluetooth 5.2 over the host Bluetooth USB signal path; verify the board cable/pin routing and install the matching Bluetooth driver.
Operating-system / driver compatibilityUse current Intel or OEM wireless/Bluetooth packages for their tested Windows 10/11 configurations. Linux support depends on kernel, firmware and OEM platform enablement; no generic driver promise is implied.
Regulatory and host caveatsAX411 must be paired with a CNVio2-capable Intel platform and matching BIOS/firmware; an M.2 Key-E-shaped slot alone is not enough. Double Connect is only available on qualified platform/software combinations. 6 GHz operation is subject to country rules, access-point support and OS/driver restrictions.

Model-specific installation notes

Install only with the host powered down and follow the manufacturer’s manual for slot, cable, antenna and ESD precautions. Fit and electrical compatibility are separate questions: the host must support the stated interface, firmware and driver for this exact SKU.

Nominal PHY rates vary with channel width, modulation, signal quality, regulatory domain, AP capabilities and concurrent client traffic. They are not measured Internet or application throughput. Confirm local 6 GHz/5 GHz channel permissions before deployment.
