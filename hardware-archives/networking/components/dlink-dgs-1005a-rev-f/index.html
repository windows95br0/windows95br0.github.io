D-Link DGS-1005A Rev F 5-Port Gigabit Desktop Switch

Five 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ-45 auto-negotiating ports, with no dedicated uplink and no PoE. This record is scoped to revision F/F1 documents; DGS-1005A revisions are not assumed electrically interchangeable.

MODEL IDENTIFICATION: DGS-1005A, hardware revision F (datasheet for F1; inspect label because revisions may differ). Match the complete model and hardware revision on the label before applying specifications.
