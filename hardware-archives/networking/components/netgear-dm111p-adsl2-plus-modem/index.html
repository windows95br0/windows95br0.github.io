NETGEAR DM111P ADSL2+ Ethernet Modem

Single-port ADSL2+ modem for a copper telephone subscriber line and Ethernet host/router handoff. This static record documents the modem-side line technology separately from its host interface and provider-dependent service rate.

Model-specific notes

ADSL2+ is a copper-loop standard, not VDSL or fiber. The line must be provisioned for compatible Annex/profile, and synchronization rate is not application throughput. Nominal signaling rates are standards/model ceilings rather than specimen test results or a promise of ISP throughput.
