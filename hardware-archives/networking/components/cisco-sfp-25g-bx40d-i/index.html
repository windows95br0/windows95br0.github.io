Host qualification and installation

Pair with a Cisco SFP-25G-BX40U-I at the opposite end; the U module transmits in the lower wavelength band and receives in the D module’s transmit band. Check Cisco TMG for exact part/host/release compatibility and confirm port mode, FEC, single-mode fiber quality and the 40km optical budget.

D and U are complementary endpoints, not interchangeable at both ends. The 40km model is distinct from Cisco’s shorter BXU-I family; use the matching BX40 pair.

Inspect and clean fiber end faces, retain dust caps during storage, observe ESD precautions and never look into an active optical port. Record the host, port, software release, module label and DOM readings where available.
