D-Link DGS-1008A Rev F 8-Port Gigabit Easy Desktop Switch

Eight auto-negotiating 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ-45 ports and no dedicated uplink, SFP, or PoE output. Do not apply these revision F figures to visually similar D-Link switches or other DGS-1008A hardware revisions.

MODEL IDENTIFICATION: DGS-1008A, hardware revision F (F1 data sheet; confirm label). Match the complete model and hardware revision on the label before applying specifications.

Model-specific notes

Eight auto-negotiating 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ-45 ports and no dedicated uplink, SFP, or PoE output. Do not apply these revision F figures to visually similar D-Link switches or other DGS-1008A hardware revisions. Switching capacity is the manufacturer’s aggregate fabric figure; it is not a benchmark.
